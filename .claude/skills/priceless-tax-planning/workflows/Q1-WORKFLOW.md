# Q1 Workflow — January through April 15

Q1 is the heaviest compliance quarter of the year. Prior-year returns come due, current-year planning baseline gets set, and several critical deadlines compress into a narrow window. The Q1 memo anchors the annual planning cycle for the client.

## What Q1 covers (calendar overview)

**January**:
- Prior-year W-2s and 1099s issued to employees and contractors (due Jan 31)
- Q4 of prior year estimated payment due (Jan 15)
- Begin prior-year return preparation

**February**:
- Continue prior-year preparation
- Begin current-year baseline projection

**March**:
- S Corp and Partnership returns or extensions due (Mar 15)
- Late S Election decisions finalized
- First quarterly memo to client (typically mid-to-late March)

**April**:
- Personal return or extension due (Apr 15)
- Q1 estimated payment due (Apr 15)
- Prior-year IRA/HSA contribution deadline (Apr 15)
- Final Q1 planning memo confirmed

## The Q1 memo's unique role

Unlike Q2, Q3, Q4 memos (which are incremental check-ins), the Q1 memo is annual anchor:

- Reconciles prior-year outcome (closes the loop on last year's plan)
- Establishes current-year baseline
- Sets Q1 estimated payment amount
- Captures any new-year events (new entities, new employees, planned transactions)
- Launches the annual planning cycle

A well-done Q1 memo sets the tone for the year. A rushed Q1 memo creates compounding problems through Q2/Q3/Q4.

## Pre-Q1 preparations (December of prior year)

Before Q1 engagement begins:

- [ ] Prior-year books closed through December
- [ ] Year-end reconciliations complete (bank, CC, loans, fixed assets)
- [ ] Prior-year payroll closed
- [ ] W-2s and 1099s prepared for issuance
- [ ] Client profile reviewed and annual updates captured
- [ ] Tax Return Analysis of prior year (Phase 0.5) anticipated — Priceless prepared to execute

## Phase sequence for Q1 engagements

Standard 5-phase flow (including Phase 0.5). Q1-specific notes per phase:

### Phase 0.5: Tax Return Analysis

Run on prior-year return AS SOON AS filed. Provides:
- Carryforwards going into current year
- Basis position entering current year
- Any errors or missed strategies from prior year
- Baseline for current-year projection

For returns on extension: Phase 0.5 runs on prior-year draft when available, updated when final.

### Phase 1: Bookkeeping QA

Q1 version covers:
- Prior-year bookkeeping QA (confirms books tie to filed return)
- Current-year Q1 bookkeeping QA (first few months)
- Payroll reconciliation for both years
- Any carryforward items from last year's QA

Expect more findings in Q1 than other quarters. Year-end often surfaces classification issues that went unnoticed.

### Phase 2: Tax Projection

Q1 projection is the foundational one for the year. Methodology:
- Prior-year actuals (just filed or being filed)
- Current-year YTD (January-March) — thin but establishes trend direction
- Remainder of year: seasonal adjustment using prior-year monthly distribution, adjusted for known events

Assumptions section in Q1 memo is the most important of the year — these assumptions carry through Q2/Q3/Q4 memos unless updated. Make them specific and verifiable.

### Phase 2.5: Payroll Analysis

For S Corp clients:
- Reasonable comp for current year — set the target EARLY
- Target set by mid-Q1 means full-year execution
- If target changes from prior year, update payroll provider this quarter

Critical: reasonable comp methodology memo dated AND board resolution adopted in Q1 for the year.

### Phase 3: Strategy

Q1-specific strategy opportunities:
- **Late S Election** — if applicable, file in Q1 (Rev. Proc. 2013-30 relief)
- **Current-year S Corp election** — Form 2553 by March 15 for calendar year
- **New entity formation** — any Q1 entity moves (Operating Co, holding company, real estate LLC)
- **Retirement plan establishment** — if new DB plan for current year, begin design
- **Accountable plan formalization** — retroactive to Jan 1 if signed early in year
- **Augusta Rule planning** — schedule meetings for the year
- **PTET election** — most states' deadlines are early Q1 or late Q2
- **Hiring children / spouse setup** — payroll configured for the year
- **Charitable contribution planning** — multi-year strategy confirmation

Forward-looking considerations (per updated Operator 5):
- 2-year view setup: is current year expected to be similar to, higher than, or lower than next year?
- Strategy decisions that benefit from that context

### Phase 4: Synthesis

Q1 memo assembly. Tier-specific sections:

**Foundational tier Q1 memo**:
- Prior-year outcome recap (1 paragraph)
- Current-year baseline projection
- Top 3-5 strategies for the year
- Q1 payment due (April 15)
- Key assumptions for client verification

**Comprehensive tier Q1 memo**:
- All of above
- Multi-entity rollup if applicable
- Financial product overlay if Path B infrastructure
- Documentation generation queue
- Annual planning calendar (dates for Q2, Q3, Q4 check-ins)

**Full Wealth tier Q1 memo**:
- All of above
- Capital Deployment evaluation
- Estate coordination notes
- 3-year planning outlook
- Affiliate coordination memo

## Critical Q1 deadlines (don't miss)

| Date | Deadline | Applies To |
|------|----------|------------|
| Jan 15 | Q4 prior-year estimated payment | All with prior-year ES obligation |
| Jan 31 | W-2 and 1099 issuance | Any employer or 1099 issuer |
| Feb 28 / Mar 31 | W-2 and 1099 IRS filing | Paper / electronic respectively |
| Mar 15 | 1120S and 1065 returns (or extension Form 7004) | S Corp and partnership clients |
| Mar 15 | New S Corp election for current year (Form 2553) | Entities electing S Corp for current year |
| Apr 15 | 1040 return (or extension Form 4868) | Most individuals |
| Apr 15 | Q1 current-year estimated payment | All with ES obligation |
| Apr 15 | Prior-year IRA / HSA contributions | All who haven't maxed |
| Apr 15 | Solo 401(k) / SEP employer contributions for prior year | Self-employed / S Corp owners |
| Apr 15 | FBAR / Form 114 filing (automatic extension to Oct 15) | Clients with foreign accounts >$10K |

State deadlines:
- Most states follow federal (Mar 15 for entities, Apr 15 for individuals)
- Some states have different dates (check per-state file when built)
- PTET election deadlines vary significantly — most Q1 or early Q2

## Q1 estimated payment calculation

Default approach: 25% of safe harbor target.

Safe harbor target = lesser of:
- 90% of current-year projected tax
- 100% of prior-year tax (110% if prior-year AGI > $150K)

Q1 payment = safe harbor target / 4.

Adjustments:
- If client had refund carryforward applied to current year, reduce Q1 payment by that amount
- If client is behind based on Q4 prior-year payment status, catch-up logic applies
- If client has W-2 withholding that already covers current year pro-rated, Q1 estimated payment may be zero

Payment options:
- IRS Direct Pay: https://www.irs.gov/payments/direct-pay
- EFTPS: https://www.eftps.gov/eftps/
- Mail check with Form 1040-ES voucher
- State portal (state-specific)

## Common Q1 findings

### From Phase 0.5 (Tax Return Analysis)

- Prior-year return had §162(l) coding error (affects current year fix)
- QBI wasn't optimized on prior year (aggregation election missed, etc.)
- Reasonable comp was low on prior year
- Basis position unclear (trigger separate engagement)
- Prior CPA errors from new-to-Priceless client (amendment considerations)

### From Phase 1 (Bookkeeping QA)

- Year-end adjusting entries not made (depreciation, accruals)
- Fixed asset additions not capitalized (expensed as supplies)
- Owner distributions miscoded (recorded as expenses)
- 1099s missing for contractors >$600

### From Phase 2 (Projection)

- Projected tax significantly higher than prior year (income growth, unfavorable changes)
- Projected tax significantly lower (income decline, new NOLs, carryforwards)
- Safe harbor requires aggressive Q1 payment due to prior-year jump
- W-2 withholding is materially off (client should adjust W-4)

### From Phase 3 (Strategy)

- Late S Election opportunity discovered
- Current-year S Corp election needed (Form 2553 deadline approaching)
- QBI aggregation election should be made on current-year return
- PTET election deadline approaching
- New retirement plan establishment opportunity

## Delivery timing

Target: Q1 memo delivered by **early to mid-March** (before 3/15 entity return deadline).

Why early:
- Client needs time to act on Q1 estimated payment (due 4/15)
- Late S Election opportunities need filing by specific dates
- PTET election deadlines often early Q2
- Engagement letter renewals and any scope changes confirmed early

If delivery slips past 4/1, Q1 payment recommendation may be under time pressure for client.

## Q1 client communication

Opening of Q1 memo often includes annual framing:

"This is our Q1 2026 planning memo — the first of the year. Two key components:

First, we've closed out 2025: here's what you paid in total tax, what carryforwards move to 2026, and what we learned that affects this year's plan.

Second, we've set your 2026 baseline and identified the strategies with the most impact for this year. You'll see recommendations for Q1 (act by 4/15), and items we'll execute in Q2, Q3, and Q4.

We'll check in quarterly — next memo is early June (Q2). As always, material changes mid-year reach out immediately."

This framing prepares the client for the annual rhythm.

## Q1-specific risks

- **Prior-year return rushed** — if analyst rushes Phase 0.5 to fit Q1 timeline, carryforwards get missed or misstated
- **Reasonable comp set too late** — if comp target not locked in by March, year-to-year consistency suffers
- **Assumptions too thin** — Q1 has limited YTD data; over-relying on run-rate creates bad projection
- **Strategy window missed** — Late S Election, PTET election, entity formation decisions have hard Q1 deadlines
- **Client not responsive** — Q1 is busy for clients too; ensure adequate lead time for client decisions

## Q1 checklist (condensed)

Before memo delivery:
- [ ] Phase 0.5 complete (Tax Return Analysis)
- [ ] Prior-year carryforwards cataloged and documented
- [ ] Phase 1 complete (Bookkeeping QA both years)
- [ ] Phase 2 complete (Projection with explicit assumptions)
- [ ] Phase 2.5 complete for S Corp clients (reasonable comp set)
- [ ] Phase 3 complete (strategies including time-sensitive Q1 items)
- [ ] Phase 4 assembled (memo with tier-appropriate sections)
- [ ] Partner review complete
- [ ] Client-facing memo converted per template
- [ ] Excel model built and shared
- [ ] Karbon tasks created from structured task block
- [ ] Payment instructions verified (live URLs, correct amounts)
- [ ] Next check-in (Q2) calendar-booked
