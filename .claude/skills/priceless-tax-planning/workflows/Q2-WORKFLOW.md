# Q2 Workflow — April through June 15

Q2 is the first incremental check-in after the annual anchor. Prior year is closed, current-year baseline is established from Q1. Q2 is about confirming trajectory and activating Q2-actionable strategies.

## What Q2 covers (calendar overview)

**April** (bleeding from Q1):
- Personal return filed or extended (Apr 15)
- Q1 estimated payment made (Apr 15)

**May**:
- YTD data becomes meaningful (4 months of actual data is usable)
- Begin Q2 engagement prep
- Strategies actionable through year-end become visible

**June**:
- Q2 estimated payment due (Jun 15)
- Q2 memo delivered by mid-month

## The Q2 memo's role

Incremental, not foundational:
- Confirms Q1 assumptions are holding
- Flags any drift from projected baseline
- Surfaces Q2-actionable strategies
- Calculates Q2 estimated payment
- Updates the year ahead based on latest info

Typical Q2 memo is shorter than Q1 — 40-60% of Q1 length.

## Phase sequence for Q2 engagements

Phase 0.5 skipped (Tax Return Analysis already done in Q1 unless client brings new entity or material amendment).

### Phase 1: Bookkeeping QA (abbreviated)

Incremental: confirms Q1 through Q2 books are clean. Shorter than Q1 QA.

Focus:
- Q2 reconciliations through May
- Any classification issues surfacing in recent transactions
- Payroll reconciliation Q1-Q2 combined
- Verification that Q1 recommendations (accountable plan, Augusta, etc.) are being implemented in books

### Phase 2: Tax Projection (updated)

Update Q1 projection with actual YTD data:
- Revenue tracking vs. projected
- Expense tracking vs. projected
- Any surprises to investigate

If actuals are close to Q1 projection (±10%): standard update, same recommendations.
If actuals materially off from Q1 projection: investigate, potentially revise strategy plan.

### Phase 2.5: Payroll Analysis (check-in)

For S Corp clients:
- Confirm reasonable comp from Q1 is being paid
- Mid-year check: are W-2 wages tracking to target?
- Any mid-year adjustments needed

### Phase 3: Strategy (incremental)

Q2-specific strategy opportunities:

**Still actionable from Q1 (if not implemented)**:
- Late S Election (past March deadline but Rev. Proc. 2013-30 allows ongoing)
- Accountable plan formalization (for remainder of year)
- Augusta Rule meetings (monthly, continue)
- Home office reimbursement setup

**Q2-specific**:
- PTET election (in states with Q2 deadlines)
- Real estate investment completion (cost seg scheduling)
- Mid-year retirement plan design for high-income clients
- §179 / bonus depreciation planning for Q3-Q4 purchases
- Summer hiring decisions (children, spouse)

**Early on Q3-Q4 items**:
- Begin planning for year-end charitable giving strategy
- Cash Balance Plan design (need 90 days before year-end to implement)
- DB plan establishment timeline

### Phase 4: Synthesis

Q2 memo assembly. Shorter than Q1.

Tier-specific:

**Foundational Q2**:
- 3-5 updates from Q1 recommendations (progress)
- Q2 payment amount
- Any new priorities
- Updated projection

**Comprehensive Q2**:
- All above, plus
- Any Capital Deployment exploration if relevant
- Separate engagement decisions if applicable

**Full Wealth Q2**:
- Comprehensive framing
- Mid-year partner review notes
- Any estate coordination updates

## Q2 deadlines

| Date | Deadline | Applies To |
|------|----------|------------|
| Apr 15 | Q1 ES, 1040, IRA/HSA (Q1 overlap) | All |
| Jun 15 | Q2 estimated payment | All with ES obligation |
| Jun 15 | Expatriate 1040 (automatic extension) | Clients abroad |
| Varies | PTET elections in various states | Per state |

Prior-year Solo 401(k) and SEP contributions: due with tax return, which was filed or extended. If extension filed, prior-year employer contribution is due by extended deadline.

## Q2 estimated payment calculation

If Q1 payment was on track: Q2 payment = 25% of safe harbor target (standard quarterly).

If behind from Q1: catch-up logic. Either add to Q2 or consider W-2 withholding adjustment.

If ahead from Q1: may be able to reduce Q2 payment (rare, but possible when client over-paid Q1).

Payment mechanism same as Q1.

## Q2 common findings

- Q1 recommendation not yet implemented (client missed the follow-through)
- Material event occurred since Q1 (unexpected income, unexpected expense)
- Client has new information about year (got the contract, closed the deal, hired the employee)
- Bookkeeping still cleaning up Q1 findings (give grace, but follow through)
- PTET election deadline approaching or just missed
- Summer hiring plans becoming concrete (Augusta, children, spouse)

## Q2 client communication

Opening framing:

"Your Q2 check-in. Short version: we're four months into 2026, here's what's on track, what's not, and what needs attention between now and September.

Q1 recommendations status:
- Accountable plan: [implemented / pending]
- Augusta meetings: [on schedule / need to catch up]
- [Specific to client]

Your Q2 estimated payment of $X is due June 15. Safe harbor status: [on track / ahead / behind].

Next memo: early September (Q3 — typically the longest of the year due to strategy window)."

Client appreciates brevity in Q2. Save the deep dive for Q3.

## Q2 delivery timing

Target: early June delivery, leaving 10-14 days before Q2 payment deadline.

## Q2-specific risks

- **Too brief**: If Q2 memo is just a check-in with no substance, clients question the fee for that engagement. Ensure it includes at least one actionable recommendation.
- **Q1 recommendations not followed through**: Be honest with client about what hasn't been done. Re-surface items that need attention.
- **Client disengagement**: Summer months, clients less responsive. Build in extra lead time.
- **YTD data still thin**: 4-5 months of data is better than 1-2 but still incomplete. Don't over-commit on projection precision.
