# Q3 Workflow — July through September 15

Q3 is the flagship strategy quarter. Six months of YTD actuals make projections reliable, and most strategies still have enough runway to execute. The Q3 memo is typically the longest and most substantive of the year.

## Why Q3 is the strategy quarter

- **YTD data is reliable**: 6 months of actual data means projections are accurate enough to justify material decisions
- **Most strategies still actionable**: Retirement contributions, entity restructuring, capital deployment, charitable planning — all still have lead time in Q3
- **Late enough to be accurate, early enough to execute**: Q4 strategies rushed; Q1/Q2 projections rely too heavily on estimates; Q3 is the sweet spot

Most of Priceless's annual tax savings are identified in Q3.

## What Q3 covers (calendar overview)

**July**:
- YTD data through end of Q2 is available
- Begin Q3 engagement prep
- First 6 months of business year fully analyzable

**August**:
- Deep strategy analysis
- Partner review of proposed strategies
- Client engagement on major decisions

**September**:
- Q3 estimated payment due (Sep 15)
- Extended entity returns due (Sep 15 for 1120S, 1065)
- Q3 memo delivered
- Major strategies committed for Q4 execution

## The Q3 memo's role

The flagship memo:
- Substantive mid-year review with reliable YTD data
- Major strategy commitments for year-end execution
- Multi-year planning conversations (per Operator 5)
- Capital deployment evaluations (for qualifying clients, AGI > $750K)
- Separate engagement decisions surfaced

Typical Q3 memo: 2-3× the length of Q2 memo. Clients may complain; partner reassures that Q3 is the one that earns the retainer.

## Phase sequence for Q3 engagements

### Phase 1: Bookkeeping QA (thorough)

More substantial than Q2 because YTD data needs to be reliable for Q3 projections:
- Full re-reconciliation (bank, CC, loans)
- Payroll through August
- Classification scan on all Q2-Q3 transactions
- Trend analysis against prior year (first half now comparable)
- Basis / AAA roll-forward check

### Phase 2: Tax Projection (comprehensive)

Q3 projection is the most accurate of the year:
- 6 months of actuals
- Well-understood seasonal pattern
- Known events for remainder of year are mostly known
- Reliable remainder-of-year estimate

Multi-year framing is key here per updated Operator 5:
- What's current year projecting to?
- What's next year looking like based on everything we know?
- Where do decisions diverge between current-year and next-year optimization?

### Phase 2.5: Payroll Analysis (mid-year check)

For S Corp clients:
- YTD W-2 wages through Aug: are we on track to year-end target?
- If off target, Q3 is time to adjust (remaining months still have material capacity)
- Year-end bonus considerations (if comp needs catch-up)
- §162(l) premium through Aug: correctly flowing
- HSA contributions on track
- Retirement plan contributions through Q3: how much capacity remains

### Phase 3: Strategy (the flagship phase)

This is where most of the annual savings get identified. All eight operators get full attention.

**Operator 1: Reconcile** — confirmed via Phase 1.

**Operator 2: Baseline** — confirmed via Phase 2 with high confidence.

**Operator 3: Reasonable Comp** — mid-year verification, any adjustments for remainder of year.

**Operator 4: Entity-Arbitrage** — still time for entity restructuring if warranted (new entity formation, check-the-box elections, holding company structure).

**Operator 5: Accelerate/Defer** — THE operator for Q3. With reliable current-year projection and visibility into next year, timing decisions are now high-leverage:
- Bonus depreciation decisions on planned Q4 asset purchases
- §179 election sizing
- Roth conversion capacity (if low-income year ending)
- Charitable bunching timing (big charity year this year or next?)
- Loss harvesting preview (do we have gains to offset in Q4?)
- Installment sale considerations for planned property sales

**Operator 6: Stack** — compound strategy opportunities:
- DB plan + Solo 401(k) + HSA for high-income clients
- DAF + QBI optimization + Roth conversion
- Reasonable comp + §162(l) + HSA + 401(k) for S Corp owner
- §1031 + cost seg + bonus depreciation for real estate

**Operator 7: Financial Product Overlay** — for Path B engagements:
- Insurance needs surfaced during engagement
- PPLI for high-tax-alpha investors
- Cash balance plan design (need 90 days before year-end)

**Operator 8: Capital Deployment** — for qualifying clients (AGI > $750K):
- Year-end equipment leasing partnerships
- Aircraft partnerships (lead time significant)
- Oil and gas working interests
- FX partnerships
- Opportunity Zones (if gains being realized)
- Charitable contribution structures

### Phase 4: Synthesis

Q3 memo is substantial. Full tier treatment:

**Foundational Q3**: Top 5-8 strategies with implementation plans for Q4
**Comprehensive Q3**: Top 8-10 strategies + financial product overlay + separate engagements surfaced
**Full Wealth Q3**: Full Comprehensive + Capital Deployment analysis + multi-year planning + estate coordination

Structured task block is largest in Q3 — most tasks kicking off for Q4 execution.

## Q3 estimated payment

Standard: 25% of safe harbor target.

With 6 months of actuals, consider switching safe harbor basis:
- If prior-year based made sense earlier in year, continue
- If current-year is now clearly lower than prior-year basis, consider switching to 90% current-year method (can reduce quarterly payment)
- Partner decision

## Major strategy decisions that happen in Q3

### Cash Balance Plan establishment

If adding CBP for current year, need plan document adopted by Dec 31. Actuary design, ERISA attorney review, custodian setup — 60-90 days. Decision must be made by early-to-mid Q3 to hit year-end.

### Defined Benefit Plan design

Same timeline as CBP. Q3 decision, Q4 execution.

### New retirement plan for current year

Solo 401(k) plan document establishment possible through tax filing deadline, but employee deferrals require plan to exist before deferral occurs — practically, before year-end.

### Entity restructure for current year

New entity formation, transfer of assets, election filings. If desired effective for current year, Q3 decision point.

### Real estate §1031 execution

45-day identification and 180-day exchange rules — must be coordinated with property sale timing.

### Capital Deployment (for qualifying clients)

Most capital deployment strategies have substantial lead times:
- Equipment leasing partnerships: subscription periods, due diligence, funding
- Aircraft partnerships: similar
- Oil and gas: subscription + operational timeline
- Opportunity Zones: 180-day rule from gain realization

Q3 is the decision window for year-end deployment.

### Charitable giving structures

- DAF funding: by Dec 31, but planning in Q3
- Appreciated asset donations: identify which positions to give
- Bunching strategy: finalize whether this is a bunch year or off year

### Roth conversion sizing

Most valuable in low-income years. Q3 visibility on year's income enables appropriate sizing before year-end.

## Q3 common findings

- Client's business trajectory clearer than Q1/Q2 estimates
- YTD bookkeeping issues require Q3 cleanup engagement
- Reasonable comp year-end true-up math becomes material
- Multi-year view reveals decision points that weren't visible earlier
- Capital Deployment opportunities surface for high-income clients
- Estate coordination issues surface (aging parents, generational planning)
- Separate engagement opportunities become concrete (cost seg on planned property, DB plan for following year)

## Q3 client communication

Framing for Q3 memo:

"Your Q3 2026 planning memo. This is our most substantive check-in of the year — six months of data means we can see where the year is landing and make the major decisions for year-end execution.

Three things most important:
1. [Biggest strategy decision — often the one with largest dollar impact]
2. [Second-tier strategy with Q4 execution]
3. [Administrative / documentation item needing attention]

Your Q3 estimated payment of $X is due September 15.

After this memo, there's limited time to execute new strategies for 2026. Q4 is primarily about implementation — setting up plans, funding contributions, making final decisions. If we're going to add [CBP / DB / major restructure / QSBS planning for exit / etc.], this memo is the commitment point.

Next check-in: early December (Q4), primarily focused on year-end execution."

Q3 is the conversation memo. Partner time on Q3 review and client call is meaningful.

## Q3 delivery timing

Target: early-to-mid September delivery.

Why: clients need time to act on:
- Q3 payment by Sep 15
- Major strategy decisions (CBP, DB, entity restructure) need 60-90 days before year-end
- Extended entity returns due Sep 15

A mid-September Q3 memo leaves only October-November for major strategy execution.

## Q3-specific risks

- **Not enough time allocated**: Q3 is not a standard-size engagement. Block extra time.
- **Partner availability**: Q3 strategy decisions need partner engagement; plan accordingly.
- **Client disengagement**: After summer, some clients busy. Build adequate response windows.
- **Over-promising on complex strategies**: QSBS, Capital Deployment, DB plan — these need real work. Don't commit to strategies we can't deliver.
- **Cost seg study scheduling**: these studies take 4-8 weeks; Q3 is last reasonable window for current-year execution.
