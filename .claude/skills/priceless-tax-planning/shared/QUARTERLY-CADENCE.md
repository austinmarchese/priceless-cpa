# Quarterly Cadence

Priceless CPA delivers tax planning on a quarterly maintenance model. This file defines what happens in each quarter, what decisions are live in each window, and when deliverables are produced. Every quarterly workflow file (`quarterly-workflows/Q1-*.md` etc.) implements the rhythm described here.

## Delivery principle

We deliver each quarterly touch **3-4 weeks before the corresponding estimated-tax payment deadline**, so the client has time to review, respond with questions, and execute recommended actions before the deadline.

## The four quarters, aligned to IRS estimated-tax quarters

The IRS estimated-tax quarters don't map cleanly to calendar quarters — Q1 runs Jan-Mar, Q2 is Apr-May (two months), Q3 is Jun-Aug (three months), Q4 is Sep-Dec (four months). Payment deadlines are 4/15, 6/15, 9/15, and 1/15 of the following year.

Our quarterly deliveries target:

| Quarter | IRS period | Payment due | Priceless delivers | Primary purpose |
|---------|------------|-------------|--------------------|------------------|
| Q1 | Jan 1 – Mar 31 | Apr 15 | mid-March | Prior year close, current year baseline |
| Q2 | Apr 1 – May 31 | Jun 15 | late May | Mid-year checkpoint, early strategy |
| Q3 | Jun 1 – Aug 31 | Sep 15 | late August | Strategy window, implementation decisions |
| Q4 | Sep 1 – Dec 31 | Jan 15 | mid-December | Year-end execution, reasonable comp true-up |

## Q1 — Close-out and baseline (delivery mid-March)

**Primary inputs expected:**
- Prior-year final books (reconciled, closed, tied to tax return)
- Prior-year tax return (draft or final, with all carryforwards identified)
- Current-year January-February YTD payroll and books
- Known events planned for current year (expected revenue changes, asset purchases, relocation, entity changes)

**Deliverable:** Q1 Tax Plan Memo

**Primary decisions:**
- Did prior-year strategies execute as planned? Document outcomes.
- What's the current-year baseline projection given prior-year close and early YTD trajectory?
- Q1 estimated payment amount (due 4/15).
- Any strategies that require early-year execution (retirement plan establishment, new entity formation for the year, S Corp election changes, PTET election registration in states where early-year elections apply).
- Any pending extension decisions for prior-year filing.

**Strategies typically actionable in Q1:**
- New retirement plan establishment (401k, DB plan effective 1/1)
- S Corp election for current year if not already (Form 2553 by 3/15 for calendar year)
- Entity formation for new ventures
- PTET election registration for current year (varies by state)
- Roth conversion decisions based on prior-year AGI finalization
- Accountable plan formalization for current year

**Out of scope in Q1:**
- Year-end timing strategies (too early)
- Cost seg on properties not yet acquired (premature)
- Aggressive income deferral to current year (already passed)

## Q2 — Mid-year checkpoint (delivery late May)

**Primary inputs expected:**
- Current-year YTD through April books (reconciled)
- Current-year YTD through April payroll
- Any life events or business events YTD
- Updated revenue pipeline or trajectory

**Deliverable:** Q2 Tax Plan Memo

**Primary decisions:**
- Is the current-year projection tracking to the Q1 baseline? Material variances get flagged.
- Q2 estimated payment amount (due 6/15).
- Early identification of strategies that need Q3 setup to execute in Q4.
- Reasonable comp check: is the YTD W-2 tracking to the annualized target?
- Any new strategies unlocked by YTD performance (e.g., business is tracking to significantly higher income than baseline — triggers review of DB plan, QBI optimization, entity restructure).

**Strategies typically actionable in Q2:**
- Reasonable comp mid-year adjustment (easier to correct now than Q4)
- Accountable plan implementation if not yet done
- HSA funding optimization
- Quarterly charitable bunching into a DAF
- Solo 401(k) contribution planning (sets the runway for Q4 funding)
- Early-stage discussion of cost seg if acquisitions are planned for Q3/Q4

**Out of scope in Q2:**
- Year-end specific moves (still too early)
- Final reasonable comp determinations (wait for Q3 data)

## Q3 — Strategy window (delivery late August)

**This is the highest-value quarter.** Q3 is when there's still enough runway to implement substantial strategies and enough data to project with confidence. Most firms under-serve this quarter. Our quarterly model makes this the anchor deliverable.

**Primary inputs expected:**
- Current-year YTD through July books (reconciled)
- Current-year YTD through July payroll
- Any strategic decisions the client has made YTD
- Anticipated Q4 events (bonuses, distributions, sales, acquisitions)

**Deliverable:** Q3 Tax Plan Memo (the flagship quarterly deliverable)

**Primary decisions:**
- Updated full-year projection with 7 months of actual data
- Q3 estimated payment amount (due 9/15)
- Strategy implementation queue for remaining year
- Reasonable comp final adjustment window (payroll must be corrected by 12/31 with enough runway)
- Cost seg study decision if property acquired or planned
- §1031 exchange window for properties sold YTD (180-day deadline enforcement)
- DAF funding or appreciated asset donation planning
- Retirement plan funding plan for remaining year
- Financial product overlay review — insurance gaps, buy-sell funding, estate liquidity

**Strategies typically actionable in Q3:**
- Cost segregation study and bonus depreciation
- §1031 exchange completion
- Defined benefit plan first funding
- Reasonable comp Q4 adjustment
- Charitable bunching implementation
- §1202 QSBS sale planning (if holding period will complete)
- Entity restructure for current year (last reasonable window)
- PPLI or CICs if under evaluation
- Key person insurance and buy-sell coverage

**Out of scope in Q3:**
- Next-year-only strategies (note for Q1 next year)

## Q4 — Year-end execution (delivery mid-December)

**Primary inputs expected:**
- Current-year YTD through October or November books
- YTD payroll through most recent completed pay period
- Confirmed Q4 events (bonuses declared, distributions timed, sales closed)
- Any late-year entity events

**Deliverable:** Q4 Tax Plan Memo (execution-focused, short)

**Primary decisions:**
- Final year-end moves that must close by 12/31
- Q4 estimated payment amount (due 1/15 of next year) OR decision to withhold via final payroll to cover safe harbor
- Reasonable comp true-up on final payroll run
- Final retirement plan funding (employee deferrals close 12/31, employer side has until extension)
- Final charitable gift execution
- Decision on current-year extension vs. file-on-time
- Preliminary setup for Q1 next year (new entities, election letters to file, retirement plans to establish effective 1/1)

**Strategies typically actionable in Q4:**
- Final W-2 bonus / reasonable comp adjustment
- Year-end 401(k) and HSA top-ups
- Charitable giving final execution
- Loss harvesting in taxable brokerage
- Roth conversion final execution
- Bonus depreciation on assets placed in service by 12/31
- Year-end PTET payment if required by state to qualify

**Out of scope in Q4:**
- Strategy identification (this quarter is execution, not exploration)
- Major structural changes (too late in the year for most)

## What changes across quarters

These things get updated at every quarterly touch:
- Full-year projection (narrows each quarter as actual data accumulates)
- Required estimated payment amount for upcoming deadline
- Safe harbor position
- Reasonable comp check
- Active strategy list (adds strategies that became relevant, drops strategies whose window closed)
- Financial product overlay review (new life events, coverage gaps)

These things get re-reviewed annually (typically Q1):
- Entity structure
- Long-term tax roadmap (3-5 year projection for growth-stage clients)
- Beneficiary designations on retirement accounts and insurance
- Estate liquidity analysis

## Engagement pricing signal

The quarterly cadence supports a productized **Annual Tax Planning Engagement** priced in the $2,500–$7,500 range depending on client complexity, billed quarterly. The skill produces the deliverable that justifies the quarterly bill.

Clients on the engagement receive all four quarterly deliverables plus reasonable ad-hoc questions between them. Scope expansions (cost seg studies, entity restructures, PR Act 60 relocation planning, PPLI implementation) are separately engaged with their own scope and fee.
