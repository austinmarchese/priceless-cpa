# West Virginia (WV) — State Tax File

West Virginia enacted **5% additional rate cut retroactive to January 1, 2026** via W. Va. Code § 11-21-4j (codified June 12, 2026) — reducing top rate from 4.82% (2025) to approximately **4.58%** for TY 2026. Part of continuing rate-reduction trajectory from pre-reform 6.5% top. **Social Security 100% exempt beginning TY 2026** per HB 4880 (2024).

Very low Priceless concentration.

## Quick reference

- **Personal income tax**: 5 brackets; top rate **4.58%** (2026; 5% cut from 4.82% 2025); further trigger-based reductions possible
- **Corporate income tax**: 6.5% flat
- **PTET**: Yes
- **Sales tax**: 6% state; local up to 1% (combined max 7%)
- **Estate tax**: None
- **Inheritance tax**: None
- **Social Security**: **100% exempt effective TY 2026** (completed phase-in from 65% in 2025)
- **Military retirement**: 100% exempt
- **Standard deduction**: $2,000 per exemption (state-specific; not federal conformity)
- **Filing deadline**: April 15 (conforms federal)

## Personal income tax — 2026 rate structure

Per § 11-21-4j (effective 6/12/2026, retroactive 1/1/2026), all rates reduced by 5% from 2025:

**Single filers (2026 approximate)**:
| Income Range | Rate |
|---|---|
| $0 - $10,000 | 2.11% |
| $10,001 - $25,000 | 2.95% |
| $25,001 - $40,000 | 3.29% |
| $40,001 - $60,000 | 3.76% |
| Above $60,000 | **4.58%** |

(2025 rates: 2.22% / 3.10% / 3.46% / 3.95% / 4.82% — these are reduced 5% for 2026)

### Personal exemption

$2,000 per exemption claimed federally (WV-specific structure; not federal standard deduction).

### Trigger mechanism (§ 11-21-4h)

WV's trigger mechanism allows automatic rate reductions when:
- General Fund surplus meets specified conditions
- Applied in August each year
- 2026 trigger already applied; August 2026 assessment will determine TY 2027 potential reductions

### Retirement income

- **Social Security**: 100% exempt TY 2026 (completing HB 4880 phase-in)
- **Military retirement**: 100% exempt (since 2019)
- **WV state/local police, sheriffs, firefighters retirement**: 100% exempt
- **WV Teachers/Public Employees Retirement**: Up to $2,000 deduction
- **Federal civilian retirement**: Up to $2,000 deduction
- **Railroad Retirement Board**: 100% exempt
- **Private pension / IRA / 401(k)**: Generally taxable
- **Seniors 65+ or disabled**: Up to $8,000 deduction from any income source

### WV retiree
**Setup**: WV resident; 68; MFJ; $50K combined pension + $40K Social Security.

**2026 analysis**:
- Social Security: 100% exempt
- Pension: $2,000 standard deduction (WV)
- Senior 65+ deduction: $8,000 × 2 = $16,000
- Taxable: $50K - $16K = $34,000

- WV tax at blended rates ≈ $900
