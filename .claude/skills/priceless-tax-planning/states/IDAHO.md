# Idaho (ID) — State Tax File

**Rapidly reducing flat-tax state**: Idaho converted to flat tax in 2023; rate reduced 5.8% → 5.695% → 5.3% via HB 40 enacted March 2025 (retroactive to 1/1/2024). Among the most aggressive rate reduction trajectories in the US.

Boise metro growth from California inbound migration has been substantial. Some Priceless relevance for CA-exodus clients choosing ID.

## Quick reference

- **Personal income tax**: 5.3% flat (retroactive to 1/1/2024 via HB 40)
- **Corporate income tax**: 5.3% flat (matches individual)
- **PTET**: Yes (Idaho Code §63-3092A); rate tracks corporate rate
- **Sales tax**: 6% state + up to 3% local (combined max 9%)
- **Estate/inheritance/gift tax**: NONE
- **Property tax**: effective 0.63% (low; homeowner exemption reduces)

## Personal income tax

**Rate: 5.3% flat** (since HB 40, retroactive to 1/1/2024)
- Prior: 5.695% (HB 521, March 2024; retroactive to 1/1/2024)
- Prior: 5.8% (2023)
- Prior: 6.0% (2022)
- Prior: 6.5% (2021 — graduated top rate)

### Starting point

ID begins with federal taxable income with ID adjustments.

### Standard deduction

ID uses federal standard deduction ($15,000 single / $30,000 MFJ 2025; $15,750/$31,500 2026).

### Capital gains

**60% exclusion** of qualifying capital gains on sale of ID-located real property, farm/ranch property, and certain business assets. Remaining 40% taxed at ordinary rate.

### Retirement income

- Social Security: fully exempt
- Military retirement: fully exempt
- Other retirement: graduated exclusion based on age and income

## PTET (Idaho Code §63-3092A)

**Rate**: 5.3% (matches corporate/individual rate)
**Elective**: Yes
**Federal SALT cap workaround**: Yes

## Corporate Income Tax

**Rate**: 5.3% flat
**Base**: Federal taxable income with ID modifications
**Apportionment**: Single sales factor

## Sales and use tax

**State rate**: 6%
**Local rates**: 0-3%
**Combined max**: 9%
**Most local areas**: 6% state only
**Resort areas (Sun Valley, McCall)**: additional local option tax (up to 3%)

**Economic nexus**:
- $100,000 in ID sales (from 2019)
- Marketplace facilitator collection required

**Food**: Subject to sales tax (ID is one of few states taxing groceries); $120 grocery credit on income tax return to offset

## Property tax

**Effective rate**: 0.63% statewide
**Primary residence exemption**: $125,000 or 50% of assessed value (whichever less) reduction for homeowners
**Circuit breaker**: For seniors and disabled

## Estate, gift, inheritance

NONE. No state estate tax since repeal 2005.

## Priceless client considerations

### CA departees to ID (Boise common)
- 5.3% flat rate vs. CA 13.3% top = significant savings
- FTB residency audit applies if moving from CA
- Boise tech growth; Micron, HP, Meta offices
- Lifestyle factors (outdoors, cost of living)

### S corp / LLC owners
- PTET at 5.3% (tracks individual)
- Federal SALT cap workaround value
- Standard business compliance

### Real estate investors
- Low property tax
- 60% capital gains exclusion on ID real estate sales
- 5.3% on rental income

### Retirees
- Social Security fully exempt
- Military retirement fully exempt
- Other pensions partial exclusion
- Moderate property tax; high sales tax burden on consumption
- Growing retirement destination
