# Tennessee (TN) — State Tax File

**Common FL-alternative destination**: Tennessee has no personal income tax (Hall Tax on interest/dividends fully repealed as of 2021) and is a growing migration destination for Priceless clients from CA, NY, and IL — particularly Nashville and the Knoxville/Chattanooga areas. Memphis is a logistics hub.

**Key differentiator from FL/TX/NV**: Tennessee imposes significant business taxes on operating entities — the Franchise and Excise (F&E) Tax applies to most entity types including **S corporations, LLCs, and partnerships**. This is a critical departure from the typical "no income tax state" pattern and a common surprise for clients assuming TN is purely tax-free for businesses.

For Priceless, TN clients typically need: (1) F&E tax compliance for operating entities, (2) business tax if $100K+ city/county revenues, (3) no state income tax planning at individual level, (4) residency establishment for inbound clients.

## Quick reference

- **Personal income tax**: NONE (Hall Tax on interest/dividends fully repealed effective January 1, 2021)
- **Excise Tax (TN business income tax)**: 6.5% on net earnings (includes S corps, LLCs, partnerships — NOT just C corps)
- **Franchise Tax**: 0.25% on net worth (post-2024 property measure repealed); $100 minimum
- **Tennessee Works Tax Act (2023, HB 323)**: $50,000 exemption from excise tax net earnings; expanded carryforward
- **F&E tax property measure REPEALED (HB 1893/SB 2103, 2024)**: effective TY 2024+; refund claims available for prior years
- **Franchise Tax $500K property exemption**: carveout for small businesses
- **Sales tax**: 7% state + up to 2.75% local (avg combined 9.55% — among highest in nation)
- **Business Tax**: $100K gross receipts threshold; classification-based rates
- **Estate/inheritance/gift tax**: NONE (inheritance tax repealed 2016)
- **Property tax**: local only; effective ~0.74% statewide
- **PTET**: NOT APPLICABLE (no individual income tax to work around)

## Personal income tax (NONE after 2021)

Tennessee has NO state personal income tax on wages, salaries, business income, capital gains, or investment income.

### Hall Tax (historical; fully repealed)

Tennessee previously imposed the "Hall Tax" on interest and dividend income (but not wages). Phase-out schedule:
- 2015: 6%
- 2016: 5%
- 2017: 4%
- 2018: 3%
- 2019: 2%
- 2020: 1%
- **2021+: 0% (fully repealed)**

Tennessee is now fully income-tax-free at individual level.

### What TN residents DO pay

- Federal income tax
- Federal FICA/SE tax
- TN sales tax on purchases (7% state + up to 2.75% local)
- TN property tax (local only)
- Business taxes if operating an entity

### What TN residents DO NOT pay

- State income tax on wages, investments, capital gains, retirement distributions
- State tax on Social Security, pensions, IRA/401(k) distributions

### Residency establishment

TN residency factors (no day-count rules since no state income tax):
- TN driver's license (obtain within 30 days per Tenn. Code Ann. §55-50-321)
- Voter registration
- Vehicle registration
- Physical presence
- TN address on professional and financial accounts

Common inbound patterns: Nashville for music/entertainment industry, Knoxville for tech (Oak Ridge national labs adjacent), Chattanooga for manufacturing, Memphis for logistics.

## Business taxes — Franchise & Excise (F&E) Tax (CRITICAL)

Tennessee's F&E Tax is a significant departure from other no-income-tax states. It applies to most entity types operating in Tennessee, including pass-throughs.

### Who pays F&E Tax

**Subject entities** (per Tenn. Code Ann. §67-4-2105):
- C corporations
- **S corporations** (NOT exempted — this is unusual compared to most states)
- **LLCs** (including single-member LLCs in most cases)
- **Limited partnerships** (LPs)
- Business trusts
- Professional LLCs and PCs

**NOT subject**:
- Sole proprietorships
- General partnerships (GPs) directly owned by individuals
- Nonprofits (if federal §501(c) and meet TN requirements)
- Certain passive investment entities
- REITs (in specific circumstances)

**Critical planning insight**: Unlike FL/NV/TX/WY, an S corporation or LLC operating in TN DOES pay state-level entity tax. This changes the entity-structuring calculus for TN clients.

### Excise Tax (6.5% on net earnings)

**Rate**: 6.5% (flat)
**Base**: Federal taxable income with TN adjustments
**New deduction** (Tennessee Works Tax Act, HB 323, 2023): $50,000 standard deduction from net earnings starting TY 2024
**Authority**: Tenn. Code Ann. §67-4-2007 et seq.

**TN adjustments to federal taxable income**:
- Federal depreciation conformity: TN conforms to §168 for assets purchased on or after 1/1/2023 (Tennessee Works Tax Act) — **major change from historic decoupling**
- §163(j) interest expense limitation: TN follows federal but allows carryforward of disallowed TN interest (Notice ET-4)
- No federal income tax deduction
- Rent paid to affiliate: limited deduction (Notice ET-9)
- PPP loan forgiveness: not taxable for TN; expenses deductible (Notice ET-12)

**Example**: LLC earns $200,000 in TN-source income
- Apply $50,000 exemption = $150,000 taxable
- × 6.5% = $9,750 excise tax

### Franchise Tax (0.25% on net worth)

**Rate**: 0.25% (25 cents per $100)
**Base**: Net worth (assets minus liabilities) — post-2024 calculation
**Minimum tax**: $100 per year
**NEW $500,000 property exemption**: from franchise tax property base (Tennessee Works Tax Act)

**Property measure REPEALED (2024)**: Historical alternative base was "greater of net worth OR book value of real/tangible property" — the property measure was repealed by HB 1893/SB 2103 (Ch. 950, Laws 2024) for TY 2024+.

**Refund opportunity (historical)**: Businesses that paid franchise tax on property measure for tax years ending on or after March 31, 2020 through TY 2023 may claim refunds via specific TN DOR process. Limited time window. [VERIFY current claim deadline; generally expired but check for late-claim provisions]

### F&E Tax filing

**Form**: FAE-170 (combined Franchise and Excise Tax Return)
**Due**: April 15 for calendar year filers (or 15th day of 4th month after fiscal year end)
**Extension**: Automatic 7-month via Form FAE-173; but payment due at original deadline
**Estimated payments**: Required if combined F&E liability ≥ $5,000

**Single sales factor phase-in** (per Tennessee Works Tax Act, HB 323):
- Previously three-factor: property + payroll + sales, with sales factor weighted
- Transitioning to single sales factor
- [VERIFY phase-in schedule via TN DOR]

### Business Tax (separate from F&E Tax)

**Who pays**: Businesses with $100,000+ gross receipts in any TN city/county (cumulative)
**State portion + local portion**: state rate varies by classification; local rates add

**Classifications (Tenn. Code Ann. §67-4-708)**:
- Class 1A (retailers): 0.08% (state) + 0.08% local typical
- Class 1B (wholesalers): 0.03125% + local
- Class 1C (commercial dealers): 0.08% + local
- Class 1D (retail food): 0.08% + local
- Class 1E (restaurants): 0.08% + local
- Class 2: 0.25% + local
- Class 3 (services): 0.4375% + local
- Class 4 (utilities): 0.1875% + local
- Class 5 (industrial): 0.0375% + local

Applies on TOP of F&E Tax for most Priceless operating clients. Double taxation at state-local level.

**Annual filing**: Form BUS-415 or classification-specific

### Hall Tax (REPEALED)

Historical tax on interest and dividends, fully phased out as of January 1, 2021. No current relevance for 2026+ planning.

## Sales and use tax

**State rate**: 7% (Tenn. Code Ann. §67-6)
**Local rates**: 1.5% to 2.75% (maximum statutory)
**Combined max**: ~9.75%
**Statewide average combined**: 9.55% (among highest in US per Tax Foundation)
**Nashville combined**: 9.25%
**Memphis combined**: 9.75%
**Knoxville combined**: 9.25%

**Food items**: Reduced state rate 4% (2024+ [VERIFY]) on food for home consumption
**Services**: Limited services taxed; primarily tangible goods
**Expansion**: 2023 Tennessee Works Tax Act extended sales tax to certain services [VERIFY specifics]

**Economic nexus (post-Wayfair)**:
- $100,000 in TN sales in prior 12-month period
- Marketplace facilitator collection required

## Property tax

**Administered at county level**; TN does not levy state property tax.

**Effective rates**:
- Statewide average: 0.74%
- Davidson County (Nashville): ~0.82% effective
- Shelby County (Memphis): ~1.25% effective
- Knox County (Knoxville): ~0.85%
- Rural counties: 0.50-0.80%

**Assessment ratio**: 25% for residential/farm property; 40% for commercial; 55% for industrial/mineral
**Homestead-type provisions**: Limited compared to FL; senior citizen and disabled veteran exemptions available

## Estate, gift, and inheritance tax

NONE at state level. 
- **Estate tax**: Never imposed
- **Inheritance tax**: REPEALED effective January 1, 2016
- **Gift tax**: Never imposed

Federal estate tax applies at $15M (post-OBBBA permanent). TN residents face federal-only estate tax burden.

## Priceless client considerations

### S corporation owners resident in TN (critical nuance)
- **F&E Tax applies at entity level**: 6.5% excise on net earnings + 0.25% franchise on net worth
- **$50,000 excise tax exemption** (Tennessee Works Tax Act) — small businesses get partial relief
- **No federal SALT cap issue**: TN F&E tax paid at entity level naturally reduces K-1 income flow-through (no PTET election needed)
- **Business Tax**: additional $100K+ revenue threshold tax

### LLC owners resident in TN
- Same F&E Tax treatment as S corps
- Default disregarded single-member LLCs may still need to file F&E depending on classification

### Construction contractors
- F&E Tax plus Business Tax
- Sales tax on materials (non-exempt)
- Property taxes

### Professional practices (doctors, lawyers)
- PLCs/PLLCs subject to F&E Tax
- Professional regulation state-specific

### Real estate investors
- No state income tax on rental income at individual level
- BUT if holding real estate in an LLC (even single-member), F&E Tax applies
- Alternative: hold in individual name or general partnership to avoid F&E
- Property tax moderate; no state transfer tax (recording fees apply; Tenn. Code Ann. §67-4-409)

### E-commerce and remote sellers
- $100K economic nexus
- 9.55% average combined sales tax rate high
- Marketplace facilitator collection

### High-income inbound from CA/NY
- Common migration pattern
- F&E Tax is the main entity-level tax surprise
- Consider entity structuring (GP vs. LLC) for passive holdings

## Multi-state mechanics

### TN resident with out-of-state income
- No TN tax to credit against (no TN individual income tax)
- File non-resident returns in other states where earned
- F&E Tax on TN-operating entities applies regardless of ownership residency

### Non-resident with TN-source income
- Individual: no TN personal income tax
- Entity (S corp, LLC, etc.): F&E Tax on TN apportioned earnings
- Sales tax if nexus

### Out-of-state entity with TN operations
- F&E Tax registration required
- Apportionment (single sales factor phasing in)

- **April 15, 2026**: F&E Tax return due (Form FAE-170) for calendar year filers
