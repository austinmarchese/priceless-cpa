# Delaware (DE) — State Tax File

**Dominant US entity formation jurisdiction**: 60%+ of Fortune 500 and most VC-backed companies are DE-incorporated. DE's combination of specialized Court of Chancery (Delaware Chancery Court), well-developed corporate case law, and flexible DGCL makes it the default C corp state.

For Priceless clients, DE relevance is typically:
- **C corp formation** for startups seeking venture capital (default DE corporation)
- **LLC formation** for holding companies (DE LLCs also popular; less restrictive than NV/WY charging order rules but still strong)
- **Trust situs** (secondary to SD for dynasty trusts; 110-year perpetuities period; excellent legal infrastructure)
- Occasional residents (Wilmington financial services; some Philadelphia-area commuters)

## Quick reference

- **Personal income tax**: Progressive 0% / 2.2% / 3.9% / 4.8% / 5.2% / 5.55% / 6.6%
- **Top bracket**: 6.6% on income > $60,000
- **Corporate income tax**: 8.7% flat
- **Franchise Tax (annual)**: $175 minimum (Authorized Shares Method) OR $400 minimum (Assumed Par Value Method); up to $250,000 maximum
- **Annual report**: $50 for corporations
- **PTET**: Yes; elective
- **Sales tax**: NONE (DE is one of 5 states with no sales tax)
- **Gross Receipts Tax**: Yes (0.0945% to 0.7468% depending on classification)
- **Estate/inheritance/gift tax**: NONE (state estate tax repealed 2017)
- **Property tax**: effective 0.57% (low)
- **Convenience of employer rule**: Yes (30 Del. C. §1158)
- **Rule against perpetuities**: 110 years (Delaware Code Annotated, 25 Del. C. Chapter 5)

## Personal income tax

### Brackets (2025/2026)

| Bracket | Income Range | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $0 - $2,000 | 0% |
| 2 | $2,001 - $5,000 | 2.2% |
| 3 | $5,001 - $10,000 | 3.9% |
| 4 | $10,001 - $20,000 | 4.8% |
| 5 | $20,001 - $25,000 | 5.2% |
| 6 | $25,001 - $60,000 | 5.55% |
| 7 | $60,001+ | 6.6% |

**Practical top rate**: Virtually all working professionals hit the 6.6% top bracket given the low threshold.

### Retirement income

- Pension exclusion: up to $2,000 (under 60) / $12,500 (60+)
- Social Security: fully exempt
- Military retirement: $12,500 exempt (60+) / $2,000 (under 60)
- IRA/401(k): taxable subject to pension exclusion

### Capital gains

Taxed as ordinary income.

## Corporate Income Tax

**Rate**: 8.7% flat
**Base**: Federal taxable income with DE modifications
**Exemption for DE holding companies**: DE does not tax income derived from out-of-state operations for corporations whose only DE activity is maintaining intangible investments
**Apportionment**: Single sales factor phase-in complete

**This is a key reason for DE corp popularity**: DE corps doing business elsewhere owe minimal DE income tax; significant CIT only for DE operating businesses.

## Franchise Tax (8 Del. C. Chapter 5)

**Applies to**: All Delaware-incorporated corporations (regardless of where they operate)
**Two calculation methods** (corporation pays the LOWER):

**Method 1 — Authorized Shares Method** (8 Del. C. §502):
- 5,000 or fewer authorized shares: $175
- 5,001 - 10,000 shares: $250
- Each additional 10,000 shares (or fraction): $85
- Maximum: $250,000

**Method 2 — Assumed Par Value Capital Method** (8 Del. C. §503):
- Based on issued shares, authorized shares, total gross assets
- Minimum: $400
- Maximum: $250,000
- Typically favorable for corporations with many authorized shares but fewer issued

**Both methods have $250,000 maximum** (for most corporations).

**For typical VC-backed startup**:
- 10,000,000 authorized shares is common
- Authorized Shares Method: 10M × $85 per 10K = $85,000 (very high)
- Assumed Par Value Method: typically much lower if assets are modest; maybe $400-$5,000
- File Annual Franchise Tax Report and choose the lower method

**Annual Report**: Additional $50 fee for corporations

**LLCs**: $300 annual tax (NO franchise tax like corps; simpler structure)

## Gross Receipts Tax

**Applies to**: Sales of goods and services in Delaware by businesses with substantial DE operations
**Rate**: 0.0945% to 0.7468% depending on business classification
**Monthly or quarterly filing**
**Exemptions**: Farming, mining, several industries

Not a true sales tax (paid by seller, not consumer at retail). De facto broad-based business tax.

## Delaware Trust Law

### Rule against perpetuities: 110 years

**25 Del. C. §503** — 110-year rule against perpetuities for DE trusts. Shorter than SD (unlimited) and NV (365 years) and WY (1,000 years). Adequate for most dynasty planning but terminates eventually.

### Delaware Court of Chancery

Special court with extensive experience in trust, corporate, and commercial disputes. Strong precedent library.

### Domestic Asset Protection Trust

**Delaware Qualified Dispositions in Trust Act** (12 Del. C. §3570):
- 4-year statute of limitations for most creditor claims
- Self-settled spendthrift trusts permitted
- Similar structure to SD, NV, WY

### Delaware Directed Trust

DE permits directed trust structures (12 Del. C. §3313). Investment advisor separate from administrative trustee. Similar to SD.

### Priceless trust situs considerations

- **SD vs. DE**: SD generally preferred for dynasty trusts (unlimited vs. 110 years); DE preferred for complex commercial trusts with potential Chancery Court disputes
- **DE privacy**: Less robust than SD; trust documents may become public in litigation
- **DE trustee costs**: Higher than SD due to larger trust companies (Wilmington Trust, PNC Delaware Trust, etc.)

## Convenience of Employer Rule (30 Del. C. §1158)

DE is one of 5 states applying convenience rule (along with NY, NJ until recent changes, PA, NE, AR). Applies to nonresidents working remotely for DE employers — wages sourced to DE unless remote work is employer necessity.

**Priceless impact**: PA residents working remotely for DE-HQ employers face dual taxation risk (similar to NJ residents working for NY employers).

## Sales and use tax: NONE

DE is one of 5 US states with no sales tax (AK, DE, MT, NH, OR). Significant competitive advantage for retail and for residents making large purchases.

## Property tax

**Effective rate**: 0.57% (low; below national average)
**Administered at county level**

## Estate, gift, and inheritance tax

NONE. DE estate tax repealed 2017 (previously had state-level estate tax with $5.49M exemption).

## Residency

Standard domicile factors.

## Priceless client considerations

### DE incorporation for startups (most common)
- Default state for VC-backed companies
- Franchise tax minimum $400-$5K with Assumed Par Value method
- Delaware Court of Chancery specialized expertise
- Board fiduciary duty precedent extensive
- Consider franchise tax methodology carefully as company grows

### DE LLC for holding companies
- $300 annual flat tax (simpler than corp franchise tax)
- Charging order protection reasonably strong (12 Del. C. §18-703)
- Less comprehensive than WY but well-developed case law

### DE trusts for estate planning
- 110-year perpetuities; adequate for most plans
- DAPT 4-year SOL
- Higher cost than SD; better suited for complex commercial trusts

### DE residents (rare for Priceless)
- 6.6% top rate on income > $60K
- No sales tax
- Low property tax
- No estate tax
- Wilmington financial/banking hub
