# Wisconsin (WI) — State Tax File

Wisconsin has a progressive 4-bracket income tax with **top rate 7.65%**. Notable features: **30% capital gains exclusion** (one of higher state capital gains exclusions), **$100,000 retirement income exclusion for age 65+** (married couples $150K), and **PTET** available for both partnerships (§ 71.21(6)) and S corps (§ 71.365(4m)).

Low Priceless concentration. Some Milwaukee/Madison clients.

## Quick reference

- **Personal income tax**: 4 brackets — 3.50% / 4.40% / 5.30% / **7.65% top**
- **Capital gains**: **30% exclusion** (Wis. Stat. § 71.05(25)); 60% exclusion for farm assets held > 1 year
- **Corporate income tax**: 7.9% flat
- **PTET**: Yes (both partnerships and S corps)
- **Sales tax**: 5% state + local up to 0.6% (combined max 5.6%); groceries exempt
- **Estate tax**: None
- **Inheritance tax**: None
- **Social Security**: Fully exempt
- **Retirement income exclusion (age 65+)**: Up to **$100,000 single / $150,000 MFJ** (favorable; higher than most states)
- **Military retirement**: 100% exempt
- **Standard deduction**: Phased out at higher incomes; 2025 max ~$13,460 single / $24,960 MFJ
- **Filing deadline**: April 15 (conforms federal)

### Capital gains exclusion (§ 71.05(25)) — FAVORABLE

Wisconsin allows **30% exclusion** of long-term capital gains from Wisconsin taxable income — one of highest state capital gains exclusions:
- General LTCG: 30% excluded from WI tax base
- Farm assets held > 1 year: **60% excluded** (favorable for agricultural clients)
- §1202 QSBS: Federal exclusion flows through + WI 30% on remainder

### Worked example

$1M LTCG:
- Federal: LTCG 20% + NIIT 3.8% = $238K
- WI tax base: $1M × 70% (after 30% exclusion) = $700K
- WI tax: $700K × 7.65% = **$53,550** (effective 5.35% on gross gain)

Compare to states taxing gains as ordinary (many): WI's 5.35% effective is favorable vs. MN's 9.85% or CA's 13.3%.

### Retirement income exclusion — FAVORABLE

Wisconsin is retirement-friendly:
- **Social Security**: 100% exempt
- **Retirement income (age 65+)**: Up to **$100,000 single / $150,000 MFJ** (from any source: pensions, IRAs, 401(k))
- **Military retirement**: 100% exempt

Combined: MFJ couple both 65+ with $200K retirement income + $50K Social Security:
- Social Security: $50K exempt
- Retirement exclusion: $150K exempt
- Taxable: $50,000 (vs. $200K federally)
- WI tax at blended ≈ $2,200

### Standard deduction

WI standard deduction is complex — phased out at higher income levels:
- 2025: approximately $13,460 single / $24,960 MFJ (full)
- Phases down with income
- Fully phased out at high income

## Pass-Through Entity Tax (PTET)

Wisconsin PTET available for both partnerships and S corporations:
- Partnerships: Wis. Stat. § 71.21(6)
- S corps: Wis. Stat. § 71.365(4m)
- Rate: 7.9% [VERIFY — may track top individual 7.65% or separate]
- Election: Annual
- Credit: Refundable to owners

### Worked example

S corp with $500K WI-source income:
- PTET: $500K × 7.9% = $39,500 at entity level [VERIFY rate]
- Federal SALT deduction: $39,500 × 37% = $14,615 federal savings

## Priceless client scenarios

### WI business sale (capital gain)
**Setup**: WI resident selling business for $2M LTCG.

**Analysis**:
- WI: $2M × 70% (30% exclusion) = $1.4M taxable
- WI tax: $1.4M × 7.65% = $107,100
- Effective WI rate on gross gain: 5.35%

**Compare to MN (9.85% + NII 1%)**: $200K+ MN tax on same gain. WI saves nearly $100K vs. MN.

### WI retiree
**Setup**: Milwaukee couple both 68; $130K combined retirement income + $48K Social Security.

**Analysis**:
- Social Security: 100% exempt
- Retirement exclusion (age 65+): $150K MFJ — covers all $130K retirement income
- Taxable: $0
- **WI tax: $0**

Very favorable for retirees at moderate retirement income levels.

### WI farmer
**Setup**: Farm operator selling 100-year-old family farm; $3M LTCG on land.

**Analysis**:
- Farm assets held > 1 year: **60% exclusion**
- WI taxable: $3M × 40% = $1.2M
- WI tax: $1.2M × 7.65% = $91,800
- Effective WI rate: 3.06% on gross gain

Wisconsin is notably favorable for agricultural clients.
