# Utah (UT) — State Tax File

Utah has a **FLAT 4.5% income tax** (reduced from 4.55% via HB 106, March 2025, retroactive to 1/1/2025). Corporate tax also 4.5% flat. Utah has been on a consistent rate-reduction trajectory — from 5% (pre-2018) progressively to current 4.5%.

**Utah PTET EXPIRED for tax years beginning after December 31, 2025** — creates planning gap for TY 2026+. Re-enactment possible.

Low Priceless concentration. Some Salt Lake City tech clients.

## Quick reference

- **Personal income tax**: **4.5% FLAT** (retroactive to 1/1/2025 per HB 106)
- **Corporate income tax**: 4.5% flat
- **PTET**: **EXPIRED after 12/31/2025** (similar to MN, OR; re-enactment possibility)
- **Sales tax**: 4.85% state + local up to 3%+ (combined varies)
- **Estate tax**: None
- **Inheritance tax**: None
- **Social Security**: Taxed as ordinary; limited exemption
- **Retirement income credit**: Up to $450 tax credit for age 65+; subject to income phase-outs
- **Standard deduction**: Conforms federal
- **Taxpayer Tax Credit**: Reduces liability up to specified amount; phased by income
- **Filing deadline**: April 15 (conforms federal)

### Standard deduction and Taxpayer Tax Credit

Utah conforms to federal standard deduction. Utah uniquely offers a **Taxpayer Tax Credit** (TTC) that reduces tax liability:
- Based on federal itemized deductions or standard deduction
- Phased out at higher income levels
- Partial replacement for old personal exemption

## Pass-Through Entity Tax (PTET) — EXPIRED

Utah PTET election **EXPIRED for tax years beginning after December 31, 2025**.

**Pre-2026**: Rate 4.5% (tracked individual); refundable credit.

**2026+**: Not available unless re-enacted. Legislative session [VERIFY current status].

**Impact**: UT pass-through owners for TY 2026 cannot use state PTET for federal SALT cap workaround. Lower 4.5% rate mitigates impact somewhat (vs. high-rate states where PTET loss is material).

## Priceless client scenarios

### UT S corp owner (pre-2026 PTET)
**Setup**: UT resident; S corp with $400K UT-source income.

**2025 with PTET**:
- PTET: $400K × 4.5% = $18,000 at entity level
- Federal SALT deduction: $18,000 × 37% = $6,660 federal savings

**2026 without PTET (if not re-enacted)**:
- Owner pays $18,000 on personal UT return
- Federal SALT cap deductibility depends on MAGI
- At moderate income: $40K SALT cap may absorb $18K; no federal impact
- At high income (MAGI > $600K): $10K SALT cap → $8K lost federal deduction = ~$3,000/year federal tax increase

### UT tech professional
**Setup**: SLC resident; $300K W-2; stock option gains.

**Analysis**:
- UT tax: $300K × 4.5% = $13,500 (flat)
- Plus any stock option gains at same 4.5%
- Effective UT rate: 4.5% regardless of income level

UT is favorable for tech professionals with variable income given flat low rate.
