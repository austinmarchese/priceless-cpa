# Kansas (KS) — State Tax File

**Two-bracket state transitioning to flat**: Kansas enacted flat-tax legislation in April 2025 to transition to a flat 4% rate, contingent on revenue growth and Budget Stabilization Fund triggers. Timing of implementation uncertain — likely 2027-2030. Current structure remains two-bracket.

Low Priceless concentration; some Kansas City metro overlap with Missouri (split metro area creates interesting residency planning).

## Quick reference

- **Personal income tax**: Two-bracket — 5.2% / 5.58% (top bracket on income > $30,000 single / $60,000 MFJ)
- **Transitioning to flat 4%**: contingent on triggers (timing uncertain)
- **Corporate income tax**: 3.5% / 6.5% (graduated)
- **PTET**: Yes (SALT Parity Act; elective); rate matches personal top
- **Sales tax**: 6.5% state + up to 4% local (combined max 10.5%); food dropped to 0% state effective 2025
- **Statewide property tax**: ELIMINATED for educational building / state institutions building funds beginning FY 2027 (SB 35)
- **Estate/inheritance/gift tax**: NONE (estate tax repealed 2010)
- **Property tax**: effective 1.34% (above national average)

## Personal income tax

### Brackets (2025/2026)

**Single filers**:
| Range | Rate |
|---|---|
| $0 - $30,000 | 5.2% |
| $30,001+ | 5.58% |

**MFJ**: roughly 2x single thresholds.

### Flat tax transition

April 2025 legislation provides for eventual transition to flat 4% rate when:
- Revenue growth targets met
- Budget Stabilization Fund maintained at required level

Timing: likely 2027+ [VERIFY current trigger status via Kansas DOR].

### Starting point

KS uses federal AGI with KS modifications.

### Retirement income

- Social Security: fully exempt (KS Social Security exemption expanded in recent reforms)
- Federal government retirement: fully exempt
- KS state/local government retirement: fully exempt
- Military retirement: fully exempt
- Private pensions/IRA/401(k): taxable at regular rates

## PTET (K.S.A. §79-32,279)

**Status**: Elective
**Rate**: 5.58% (matches personal top; will track rate changes)
**Federal SALT cap workaround**: Yes

## Corporate Income Tax

**Rates**:
- $0-$50,000: 3.5%
- $50,001+: 6.5% (standard rate 4% + surtax 2.5%)

**Apportionment**: Single sales factor

## Sales and use tax

**State rate**: 6.5%
**Local rates**: 0-4%
**Combined max**: 10.5%

**Food tax REDUCTION**:
- 2022: 6.5% state rate on food
- 2023: 4%
- 2024: 2%
- **2025+: 0% state rate on grocery food** (local may still apply)

**Economic nexus**: $100,000 in Kansas sales (from 2021)

## Property tax

**Effective rate**: 1.34% (above national average)
**Statewide property tax (education + institutions)**: ELIMINATED beginning FY 2027 per SB 35 (April 2025) — shifted to state general fund; local rates remain

## Estate, gift, inheritance

NONE. Kansas estate tax repealed 2010.

## Priceless client considerations

### Kansas City metro (split with MO)
- KS side (Overland Park, Lenexa, Olathe) — 5.58% top
- MO side (Kansas City MO, Independence) — 4.7% top (MO lower)
- Cross-border commuters navigate MO/KS differential
- Residency planning matters for significant income disparity

### S corp / LLC owners
- PTET 5.58% captures SALT cap value
- Transition to flat 4% if triggers met would reduce rate

### Retirees in KS
- Social Security fully exempt
- Military + government retirement fully exempt
- Private retirement taxable
- High property tax
- No estate/inheritance tax
