# Virginia — State Tax Reference

## The essentials

Virginia has a graduated income tax with a modest top marginal rate of 5.75% kicking in at just $17,000 of income (one of the lowest bracket thresholds in the country) — meaning most Virginia filers hit the top bracket.

Virginia's PTET program is notable for:
- **Short-term extension pattern**: The PTET was originally set to sunset January 1, 2026; extended one year to January 1, 2027 in response to OBBBA. This "incremental extension" approach creates planning uncertainty
- **Refundable credit** (favorable mechanic)
- **Electronic filing MANDATORY** — no paper option and no waiver
- **Automatic 6-month extension** for Form 502PTET (unusual; no separate extension application needed)

Virginia is a significant Priceless client geography — Northern Virginia (NOVA) is a tech and federal government contracting hub with many high-income households; Richmond has professional services and finance concentration; Virginia Beach and Hampton Roads have military/defense contractor ties.

For Priceless, VA clients typically need: (1) PTET election analysis (standard at 5.75%), (2) active monitoring of sunset date (currently January 1, 2027), (3) federal employee retirement planning (many NOVA retirees), and (4) DC/MD/VA multi-state commuter analysis.

## Personal income tax

### Rate structure (2026)

**Graduated brackets** (same for single and MFJ — VA doubles standard deduction but not brackets):

| Rate | Bracket (over) |
|---|---|
| 2.00% | $0 |
| 3.00% | $3,000 |
| 5.00% | $5,000 |
| 5.75% | $17,000 |

Virginia has NOT made the flat-tax conversion trend seen in neighboring states. Top bracket starts at only $17,000 — essentially a de facto flat 5.75% rate for middle and upper income.

Verified via Tax Foundation (February 2026) and VA Department of Taxation.

### Standard deduction (2026)

- Single: $8,750
- MFJ: $17,500
- [VERIFY 2026 indexed amounts]

Virginia doubled its standard deduction in 2024 legislation as part of tax competitiveness reform.

### Personal exemption

- $930 per taxpayer (2025 amount; [VERIFY 2026])
- $930 per dependent (standard)

### No retirement income exclusion (modest benefit for age 65+)

- Social Security: FULLY exempt
- Age 65+: $12,000 per person exemption for retirement income (phases out for higher income)
- Military retirement: partial exclusion for age 55+; phase-in schedule
- No blanket retirement income exclusion like GA ($65K) or IL (all)

### Residency rules

VA uses domicile + statutory residency per Va. Code §58.1-302:

**Resident**:
- Domiciled in VA, OR
- Actual physical presence in VA for > 183 days during tax year

**Statutory residency**: 183-day rule + physical presence creates resident status even without domicile

**Domicile factors**: Standard physical presence + intent factors

**Audit posture**: Moderate. VA DOR does audit departures particularly to FL/TX but less aggressively than NY/CA/MA.

**NOVA-DC-MD commuter complexities**: See multi-state mechanics section.

## Business taxes

### Corporate income tax

**Rate**: 6% flat (since 1972 — unusually stable)

**Apportionment**: Single sales factor with three-factor apportionment election for manufacturers (Va. Code §58.1-408)

**Filing**:
- Form 500 (C corp)
- Due: 15th day of 4th month after year-end (April 15 for calendar year)
- Automatic 6-month extension (Va. Code §58.1-393 — no application needed)

### S corporation treatment

- VA recognizes federal S election
- Form 502 for S corps (pass-through; no entity-level S corp tax unlike CA)
- Form 502PTET if PTET election made

### PTET — Elective Pass-Through Entity Tax

**Authority**: Va. Code §58.1-390.1 et seq. (enacted 2022 via HB 1121/SB 692)

### Sunset date and extension (CRITICAL PLANNING ISSUE)

**Original sunset**: January 1, 2026
**Current sunset (extended 2025 legislation)**: January 1, 2027
**Status of further extension**: [VERIFY 2026 General Assembly session for possible further extension]

The "incremental extension" pattern creates planning uncertainty. Commonwealth has not committed to multi-year or permanent PTET despite OBBBA preserving federal SALT cap.

### Rate

**5.75% flat on PTE taxable income** (matches top individual marginal rate; tracks future rate changes automatically)

### Eligibility

**Expanded eligibility** (via 2023 legislation HB 1456/SB 1476):
- Partnerships (any ownership structure)
- S corporations
- Removed prior requirement of "100% natural person owners" — now any PTE can elect
- But: only the PRO RATA SHARE of eligible owners is subject to PTET (non-eligible owners' share excluded from base)

**Eligible owner** = direct owner who is:
- Natural person subject to VA individual income tax
- Estate or trust subject to fiduciary income tax
- NOT: corporations, partnerships, or other non-natural persons

### Election mechanics (unusual Virginia approach)

**Election is made by doing any of the following**:
- Making an estimated PTET payment
- Making an extension PTET payment
- Filing Form 502PTET

**Deadline**: Form 502PTET must be filed on or before the extended due date of the return (October 15 for calendar year, given automatic 6-month extension)

**Revocation**: Can revoke DURING estimated/extension payments, but once Form 502PTET filed, election is BINDING for that year

**Mandatory electronic filing**: Form 502PTET must be filed electronically; no paper waiver available

### Estimated payments (post-2022 requirement)

**Required if**: PTET liability expected to exceed $1,000
**Schedule**: Quarterly (April 15, June 15, September 15, January 15)
**Form**: PTET-PMT eForm or ACH credit

**Safe harbor**: Greater of:
- 100% of prior year PTET liability (if 12-month prior year)
- 100% of current year liability based on prior year return (annualized)

### Credit to owners — REFUNDABLE (favorable)

**Refundable credit** to eligible owners equal to their share of PTET paid. Unlike MA's 90% rule or states with non-refundable credits, Virginia provides full 100% refundable credit.

**Claimed on**:
- Form 760 (individual) via Schedule VK-1 information
- Schedule CR (credit schedule)

### Post-OBBBA SALT cap interaction

With VA 5.75% rate + OBBBA $40,400 cap + $505K phase-out:

**VA residents below $505K MAGI**: $40,400 cap absorbs most VA tax for middle income; PTET marginal

**VA residents > $505K MAGI**: SALT cap phase-down makes PTET valuable
**VA residents > $565K MAGI**: SALT cap floored at $10K; PTET essential

**Typical NOVA S corp owner, $750K AGI, $40K VA tax (5.33% blended rate)**:
- Without PTET: portion of $40K fits within cap after phase-down; significant federal benefit lost
- With PTET: $43K entity-level tax (5.75% × $750K); fully federally deductible
- Net PTET federal benefit: approximately $12-16K at 37% federal bracket

## Real estate considerations

### VA Grantor Tax
**Rate**: $0.50 per $500 of consideration (0.1%) — grantor pays on deeds
**Plus Recordation Tax**: additional local fees

### Property tax
**Average effective rate**: ~0.80% of market value (below national average)
**Fairfax County (NOVA)**: 0.95-1.1%
**Arlington**: 0.85-0.9%
**Richmond metro**: 0.80-1.0%

**No homestead exemption** at state level (limited local programs)

### §1031 Exchanges
VA conforms to federal §1031; standard mechanics apply.

## Estate, gift, and inheritance taxes

**VA estate tax**: REPEALED effective July 1, 2007 (Chapter 3, 2006 Acts of Assembly)
**VA inheritance tax**: None
**VA gift tax**: None

Federal estate tax applies at $15M exemption. VA residents face federal-only estate tax burden.

## Sales and use tax

**State rate**: 5.3% (4.3% state + 1% local)
**Northern Virginia and Hampton Roads additional**: 0.7% regional tax = 6.0% total
**Grocery tax**: reduced 2.5% rate

**Economic nexus (Wayfair)**:
- $100,000 in VA sales OR 200+ transactions (Va. Code §58.1-612)
- Marketplace facilitator collection required

## Virginia-specific credits relevant to Priceless clients

### R&D Tax Credits (two tracks)

**Basic R&D credit (Va. Code §58.1-439.12:08)**:
- 15% of first $300,000 of VA qualified research expenses
- Alternative simplified method available

**Major R&D credit (Va. Code §58.1-439.12:11)**:
- 10% of VA QREs exceeding base amount
- For businesses with $5M+ VA QREs

### Historic Rehabilitation Tax Credit

- 25% of qualified rehabilitation expenses for certified historic structures
- **$7.5M maximum per taxable year per taxpayer** (increased from $5M for TY 2025+)
- Transferable credit market

### Virginia Housing Opportunity Tax Credit

- Extended to January 1, 2031 (extension from original 2025 sunset)
- Affordable housing development

### Green and Alternative Energy Job Creation Tax Credit

- Renamed 2023 from Green Job Creation Tax Credit
- Includes methane extraction in Planning District 2

### Recyclable Materials Processing Equipment Tax Credit

- Extended to January 1, 2027 (from 2025)

## Multi-state mechanics — DC/MD/VA complexities

### DC-VA-MD reciprocity and filing

**VA resident working in DC or MD (common for NOVA)**:
- DC: NO reciprocity with VA; DC taxes wages earned in DC
- MD: Reciprocity agreement — VA residents working in MD taxed only by VA (MD does NOT withhold from MD wages earned by VA residents)
- Complex for remote workers; see below

### NOVA remote worker post-COVID

VA applies standard sourcing for services performed in VA. Remote workers for out-of-state employers creates sourcing complexity — if work is physically performed in VA, VA claims taxation rights.

### VA resident with out-of-state income
- VA taxes worldwide income at graduated rates
- Schedule OSC for Other State Credit
- Credit limited to lesser of other state tax or VA tax on that income

### Non-resident with VA-source income
- Form 763 (non-resident)
- VA-source: services performed in VA, real estate in VA, VA business allocation

### Partial-year residents
- Form 760PY
- Allocations based on residency period
