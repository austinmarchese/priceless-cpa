# Rhode Island (RI) — State Tax File

Rhode Island has a progressive 3-bracket income tax (3.75% / 4.75% / **5.99%** top) with uniform brackets across filing statuses. **Estate tax threshold $1.77M** (among lowest in US; 0.8-16% rates). **7% sales tax** with no local adds. New **whole-home short-term rental tax** effective 2026.

Uniform brackets across filing statuses is unusual — MFJ does NOT get doubled bracket thresholds.

Very low Priceless concentration.

## Quick reference

- **Personal income tax**: 3 brackets — 3.75% / 4.75% / **5.99% top**; brackets UNIFORM across filing statuses
- **Corporate income tax**: 7% flat (combined with $400 minimum)
- **PTET**: Yes (§ 44-30.1)
- **Sales tax**: 7% state; no local; groceries and clothing exempt
- **Estate tax**: **YES — $1.77M threshold** (approximate 2024; indexed annually); 0.8-16% graduated
- **Inheritance tax**: None
- **Social Security**: Partially exempt (AGI-based phase-out: full exempt < $100,300 single / $125,350 MFJ)
- **Retirement income**: Up to $20,000 exclusion (Social Security retirement age+; federal AGI limits)
- **Standard deduction 2026**: ~$10,550 single / ~$21,100 MFJ
- **Filing deadline**: April 15 (conforms federal)

## Personal income tax

### 2026 brackets (uniform across filing statuses)

| Income Range | Rate |
|---|---|
| $0 - $82,050 | 3.75% |
| $82,051 - $186,450 | 4.75% |
| Above $186,450 | **5.99%** |

**Unusual**: Single, MFJ, HoH all use SAME thresholds (not doubled for joint).

### Personal exemption phase-out

$4,850 per person (2026); phases out at federal AGI $261,000; fully phased at $290,800.

### Retirement income

- **Social Security**: Fully exempt if federal AGI < $100,300 single / $125,350 MFJ
- **Retirement modification**: Up to $20,000 exclusion for taxpayers who reached Social Security retirement age (phase-out above income limits)

### Capital gains

Taxed as ordinary; top rate 5.99%.

## Pass-Through Entity Tax (PTET)

- **Rate**: 5.99% (top individual)
- **Election**: Annual; made on entity return
- **Credit**: Refundable to owners

## Corporate income tax

- **Rate**: 7% flat
- **Minimum tax**: $400
- **Apportionment**: Single sales factor (implemented 2014)

## Estate tax — $1.77M threshold

Among the lowest US estate tax thresholds. Graduated 0.8% to 16% above exemption.

### Worked example — $5M RI estate

- Taxable: $5M - $1.77M = $3.23M
- Tax: approximately $350,000-$500,000 (graduated rates) [VERIFY exact schedule]

Significant planning issue for RI homeowners (Providence home values + retirement savings often near threshold).

- **Whole-home short-term rental tax**: New tax on complete-home STRs (Airbnb, VRBO) effective 2026
- **R&D Expense Credit**: Carryforward extended from 7 to 15 years

## Priceless client scenario

### RI homeowner retirement planning
**Setup**: RI resident; $2.5M home + $800K retirement + $400K other = $3.7M estate.

- RI estate tax (if not planned): ($3.7M - $1.77M) × ~10% blended = ~$193,000
- Planning options: AB trusts (both spouses' exemptions), lifetime gifting ($19K federal annual exclusion), relocation to no-estate-tax state
