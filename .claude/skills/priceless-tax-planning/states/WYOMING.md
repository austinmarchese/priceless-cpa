# Wyoming (WY) — State Tax File

**The original LLC state** (first US LLC statute, 1977) and a top asset protection jurisdiction. Wyoming is most commonly a formation destination for Priceless clients wanting an LLC structure for privacy, asset protection, and low annual costs — not a residence destination (except some Jackson Hole / Teton County HNW clients).

Wyoming shares much with South Dakota in the trust/estate planning domain but is generally used for:
- **Wyoming LLCs** for anonymous ownership, low annual fees, and strong charging order protection (Wyo. Stat. Ann. §17-29-503)
- **Wyoming trusts** (1,000-year perpetuities period; less restrictive than SD's unlimited but still attractive)
- **Wyoming close LLCs** for family-owned businesses
- **Wyoming Series LLCs** (for separate asset silos within single entity)
- **Jackson Hole residence** for HNW clients relocating from CA/NY

## Quick reference

- **Personal income tax**: NONE (Wyo. Const. Art. XV; requires voter approval)
- **Corporate income tax**: NONE
- **Franchise tax**: NONE
- **Sales tax**: 4% state base; + local up to 2% (combined max 6%; statewide avg ~5.44%)
- **Use tax**: same as sales tax
- **Annual Report License Tax**: $60 minimum (for LLCs/corps with ≤$300K Wyoming assets); 0.0002 × Wyoming assets above threshold
- **LLC formation**: $100 filing fee
- **Estate/inheritance/gift tax**: NONE
- **Property tax**: local; average effective 0.61% (low)
- **Charging order protection**: STRONGEST in US for single-member LLCs (Wyo. Stat. Ann. §17-29-503)
- **Trust perpetuities**: 1,000 years (Wyo. Stat. Ann. §34-1-139)
- **PTET**: NOT APPLICABLE

## Personal income tax (NONE)

Wyoming has never imposed a personal income tax. The Wyoming Constitution (Art. XV) effectively requires voter approval to impose new tax forms. No state income tax return.

### What WY residents pay
- Federal income tax
- Federal FICA/SE tax
- WY sales tax on purchases
- Local property tax
- Annual Report License Tax for entity owners

### What WY residents DO NOT pay
- State income tax on any form of income
- State tax on capital gains, retirement distributions, investment income

### Residency establishment

Standard factors — no day-count rules for state tax since no state income tax. WY residency for departing-state audit purposes requires same documentation as other no-tax states:
- WY driver's license
- Voter registration
- Vehicle registration
- Physical presence
- WY address for accounts

**Jackson Hole pattern**: Teton County (Jackson area) is the primary HNW residence concentration. Very low effective property tax. CA/NY departees common (competing with FL, NV, TX).

## Wyoming LLC (CRITICAL for Priceless asset protection)

Wyoming's LLC statute is the most developed in the US and offers the strongest asset protection available in any state. Common Priceless use cases:

### Charging order exclusive remedy (Wyo. Stat. Ann. §17-29-503)

**What it means**: A personal creditor of an LLC member cannot:
- Force distributions from the LLC
- Foreclose on the membership interest
- Force dissolution of the LLC
- Attach LLC assets

The creditor can only obtain a "charging order" (right to receive distributions IF the LLC makes them). Since the member controls distributions (especially in single-member LLC), creditor effectively locked out.

**Strongest in US for single-member LLCs**: Most states' charging order statutes explicitly fail for single-member LLCs (since there are no other members to protect). Wyoming specifically applies the exclusive remedy to single-member LLCs.

**Practical impact for Priceless Full-Wealth clients**:
- Professional with liability exposure (doctors, real estate investors) uses WY LLC to hold valuable assets
- Real estate held in WY LLC; if personal lawsuit against owner, creditor cannot seize property
- Investment portfolio held in WY LLC; charging order only (distributions controlled by member)

### Privacy / anonymous ownership

Wyoming's LLC formation does NOT require member/manager names to be publicly disclosed in Articles of Organization or Annual Report. Only registered agent information is public.

**Privacy mechanism**:
- Articles of Organization: list only organizer name (often the attorney or formation service)
- Annual Report: list only manager/member if organized as manager-managed; can name a nominee
- No ongoing disclosure of true beneficial owners at state level
- **Federal CTA reporting** (Beneficial Ownership Information under Corporate Transparency Act) separately applies at federal level since 2024

For Priceless clients concerned about public disclosure (e.g., high-profile individuals), WY LLC structure provides state-level anonymity while complying with federal CTA.

### Cost

- **Formation**: $100 filing fee + registered agent ~$100/year + optional formation service
- **Annual report license tax**: $60 minimum for LLCs with ≤$300K Wyoming assets
- **Annual total (minimum)**: ~$160-$200 including registered agent
- **Compared to Delaware**: $300+ annual franchise tax vs. WY $60 minimum

### Series LLC

Wyoming permits Series LLCs (since 2017): a single LLC with multiple "series" (cells), each with separate liability from others. Cost-effective for multi-property real estate investors or multi-business owners.

- **Master LLC**: $100 filing fee + $60 annual
- **Each series**: $10 per series (one-time; per Wyoming statute)
- **Advantage**: Separate liability pools without separate filing/compliance for each

[VERIFY current Series LLC treatment under CTA — federal reporting may require each series separately]

### Close LLC

Wyoming also permits Close LLCs — fewer formalities required (no annual meetings, no board-style governance). Useful for family-owned or single-owner operations.

## Wyoming Trust Law

Wyoming has increasingly competitive trust laws, though generally considered second-tier to South Dakota for most advanced structures:

### Perpetuities: 1,000 years

Wyo. Stat. Ann. §34-1-139 establishes a 1,000-year rule against perpetuities (longer than most states; shorter than SD's unlimited). Practical effect for dynasty trusts: similar to unlimited for planning purposes (no one alive will see termination).

### Self-Settled Spendthrift Trust

Wyoming permits self-settled asset protection trusts (Wyo. Stat. Ann. §4-10-505):
- **4-year statute of limitations** for creditor challenges (longer than SD's 2-year and NV's 2-year)
- This is both an advantage (longer sleep-tight period after funding) and disadvantage (longer creditor exposure) depending on perspective
- Wyoming protector provisions available

### Directed Trust

Wyoming permits directed trust structures similar to SD. Trust company administrative role; investment advisor/family member directs investments.

### Priceless trust situs considerations

**Wyoming advantages over SD**:
- Lower cost of trust administration
- 1,000-year perpetuities sufficient for most multi-generational planning
- Less crowded; some attorneys prefer WY due to lower competition

**Wyoming disadvantages vs. SD**:
- 4-year DAPT SOL vs. SD 2-year (longer wait for strongest protection)
- Less case law than SD
- Fewer trust companies specializing in HNW/UHNW administration

For most Priceless Full-Wealth clients, SD is preferred trust situs. WY is a solid alternative for clients wanting lower administrative costs or personal ties to WY.

## Business taxes

### No corporate income tax

Wyoming does not impose any corporate income tax. C corps, S corps, LLCs, partnerships pay no state income tax on business earnings.

### No franchise tax (pure)

Wyoming does not impose a traditional franchise tax. However:

### Annual Report License Tax

**Formula**: Greater of $60 OR $0.0002 per dollar of Wyoming assets (two-tenths of one mill)
**Calculation**: Based on assets "located and employed" in Wyoming
**Minimum**: $60 for entities with ≤$300,000 Wyoming assets (because $300K × 0.0002 = $60)

**Examples**:
- $200,000 Wyoming assets: $60 (minimum)
- $500,000 Wyoming assets: $100 ($500K × 0.0002)
- $1,210,000 Wyoming assets: $242

**Due date**: First day of LLC/corporation's anniversary month (month of original formation)
**Filing**: Wyoming Secretary of State online; ~$2 convenience fee for online payment

**For LLCs primarily holding assets outside Wyoming**: Only Wyoming-sited assets count; typically minimal. For a WY LLC holding California real estate, the "Wyoming assets" = zero, so $60 minimum applies.

### Business license

Wyoming does NOT require a general state business license for most LLCs or corporations. Some industries (contractors, financial services, etc.) may need specific state licenses. Cities (Cheyenne, Casper, Jackson) may require local business licenses for certain businesses.

### Sales and use tax

**State rate**: 4% (Wyo. Stat. Ann. §39-15)
**Local rates (counties)**: 0-2% additional; most counties 1%
**Combined max**: 6%
**Statewide average**: 5.44%
**Teton County (Jackson)**: 6% combined

**Economic nexus (post-Wayfair)**:
- $100,000 in WY gross sales OR 200 transactions (Wyoming adopted economic nexus legislation post-Wayfair)
- Marketplace facilitator collection required

### Unemployment insurance

Wage base and rates administered by Wyoming Department of Workforce Services. Standard employer-only structure.

## Post-OBBBA conformity matrix

N/A at state level — no income tax at individual or corporate level.

## Property tax

**Local only** (state does not levy).

**Effective rates**:
- Statewide average: 0.61%
- Teton County (Jackson): ~0.40% effective (one of lowest in US on high-value property)
- Laramie County (Cheyenne): ~0.60%
- Natrona County (Casper): ~0.65%

**Assessment ratio**: 11.5% for residential/commercial; 9.5% for industrial; 100% for minerals
**Actual tax**: Assessment value × local mill rate

Wyoming property tax is remarkably low for high-value real estate — a primary attractor for Jackson Hole HNW residents.

## Estate, gift, and inheritance tax

NONE. Wyoming has no estate tax, no inheritance tax, no gift tax.

Federal estate tax applies at $15M (post-OBBBA permanent). WY residents face federal-only estate tax burden.

## Priceless client considerations

### LLC formation for asset protection
- Primary Priceless use case for Wyoming
- $100 formation + $60-100 annual + registered agent
- Best charging order protection in US for single-member LLCs
- Anonymous ownership at state level (CTA federal reporting still required)

### Jackson Hole / Teton County residents
- HNW relocation destination from CA, NY
- Extremely low effective property tax on high-value real estate
- No state income tax
- Lifestyle factor (skiing, national park access)
- Some Priceless Full-Wealth clients

### Trust situs (secondary to SD)
- 1,000-year perpetuities adequate for most dynasty planning
- 4-year DAPT statute of limitations (longer than SD)
- Lower trust administration costs than SD
- Consider for family with WY ties or multiple real estate holdings in WY

### Real estate investors
- No state income tax on rental income
- Low property tax
- No state transfer tax
- Real estate held in WY LLC = strong liability protection
- Common structure: real estate in other states held by WY holding LLC

### Retirees
- No state income tax on retirement distributions
- No estate/inheritance tax
- Moderate to low cost of living (except Jackson area)
- Cold winters a factor

## Multi-state mechanics

### WY resident with out-of-state income
- No WY tax to credit against
- File non-resident returns in other states

### Non-resident forming WY LLC
- Very common; Wyoming is most popular LLC formation state for out-of-state residents
- WY LLC itself pays $60/year + registered agent
- **Foreign registration required** in state where LLC operates (nexus rules of operating state apply)
- LLC income flows through to non-resident owner based on owner's home state tax rules
- No WY tax on LLC income (since no WY income tax)

**Common Priceless structure**:
- FL-resident client forms WY LLC to hold rental real estate in NJ
- NJ has state income tax; WY LLC files NJ nonresident return and/or composite
- FL resident taxed at federal level; no state income tax in FL
- WY LLC primary value: asset protection via charging order rules (though NJ courts would apply NJ law in NJ proceedings; jurisdictional analysis critical)

### LLC operating in another state
- Must register as foreign LLC in operating state (typically $100-$300 filing + annual fees)
- Operating state's tax rules apply to LLC's operating income
- WY LLC annual $60 still due regardless

## Key dates for 2026 engagements

- **First day of LLC/corp anniversary month**: Annual Report License Tax due
- **No state tax filing deadlines** for individuals (no state income tax)
- **Sales tax returns**: Filed per frequency assigned by Wyoming DOR (monthly, quarterly, or annually)
