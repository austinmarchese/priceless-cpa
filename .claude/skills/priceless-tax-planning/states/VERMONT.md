# Vermont (VT) — State Tax File

Vermont has a progressive 4-bracket income tax with **top rate 8.75%** — among higher top rates in US. **Vermont is notably one of few states WITHOUT a PTET** (along with DE, ND) — VT pass-through owners lack federal SALT cap workaround available to residents of most other states.

Vermont estate tax: $5M exemption; 16% flat rate.

Very low Priceless concentration.

## Quick reference

- **Personal income tax**: 4 brackets — 3.35% / 6.60% / 7.60% / **8.75% top**
- **Corporate income tax**: 6% / 7% / 8.5% (graduated)
- **PTET**: **NOT available** (one of few states without PTET)
- **Sales tax**: 6% state + 1% local option = 7% max
- **Estate tax**: YES — $5M exemption; 16% flat rate
- **Inheritance tax**: None
- **Social Security**: Partially exempt (income-based phase-out)
- **Standard deduction**: Starts from federal taxable income
- **Filing deadline**: April 15 (conforms federal)

## Personal income tax

### 2025/2026 brackets (inflation-adjusted annually)

**Single filers (2025 approximate)**:
| Income Range | Rate |
|---|---|
| $0 - $45,400 | 3.35% |
| $45,401 - $110,050 | 6.60% |
| $110,051 - $229,550 | 7.60% |
| Above $229,550 | **8.75%** |

### Starting point: Federal taxable income

Vermont uses federal taxable income (not federal AGI) as starting point. This means federal standard deduction flows through. OBBBA post-2025 standard deduction applies by default.

### Capital gains

- **40% exclusion** for long-term capital gains on flat property (specific qualification) OR
- **Alternative: $5,000 flat exclusion**

### Retirement income

- Social Security: Partial exemption based on AGI
- Pension income: Limited exemptions; generally taxed

## Pass-Through Entity Tax — NOT AVAILABLE

Vermont has NOT enacted a PTET. This is a planning gap:
- VT pass-through owners cannot elect entity-level state tax
- Federal SALT cap limits deductibility of state tax paid personally
- For VT owners with $40K+ state tax, $40K OBBBA SALT cap (2026) absorbs most; $10K cap (2030+) will be limiting

**Priceless action**: For VT clients with high state tax burden, no state workaround available. Monitor legislative efforts to enact PTET.

## Vermont estate tax

- **Exemption**: $5,000,000 (not inflation-indexed)
- **Rate**: **16% flat** on amount above exemption (unusual — most states have graduated rates)
- **No portability**

### Worked example — $8M VT estate

- Taxable: $8M - $5M = $3M
- VT tax: $3M × 16% = $480,000

Significant tax. Federal $15M exemption means no federal tax on $8M estate; VT takes $480K.

## Priceless client scenario

### VT business owner — no PTET workaround
**Setup**: VT resident; $500K S corp income.

**Analysis**:
- VT tax: $500K at blended rates reaching 8.75% top → approximately $37,000
- No PTET available → owner pays personally
- Federal SALT cap: $40K (2026) at MAGI < $500K absorbs; $10K if MAGI > $600K → up to $27K lost federal deduction
- **Lost federal deduction**: ~$10,000/year federal tax cost for high-MAGI owners
