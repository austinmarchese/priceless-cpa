# Iowa (IA) — State Tax File

**One of the most dramatic state tax reforms in US history**: Iowa went from 9-bracket graduated top rate 8.53% (2022) to flat 3.8% (2026) in three years. Full retirement income exemption for age 55+ (effective 2023). Estate tax eliminated 2022; inheritance tax fully phased out to 0% effective January 1, 2025.

Low Priceless concentration. Some multi-state clients; Des Moines insurance industry.

## Quick reference

- **Personal income tax**: 3.8% flat (2026; statutory permanent 3.9%; 2026 is transition-year reduction)
- **Corporate income tax**: 7.1% top rate; 5.5% for lower tier (transitioning to flat by triggers)
- **PTET**: Yes (Iowa Code §422.25C); composite nonresident tax required
- **Sales tax**: 6% state + up to 1% local (combined max 7%)
- **Estate tax**: NONE (eliminated 2022)
- **Inheritance tax**: NONE (fully phased out to 0% effective 1/1/2025)
- **Gift tax**: NONE
- **Retirement income exemption**: ALL retirement income (Social Security, pensions, IRA, 401(k), military) fully exempt for age 55+ (HF 2317, effective 2023)
- **Property tax**: effective 1.50% (above national average)
- **Filing deadline**: April 30, 2026 (NOT April 15 — Iowa statutory deadline)

## Personal income tax

**Rate trajectory (one of fastest reductions in US history)**:
- 2022: 8.53% top (9-bracket graduated)
- 2023: 6% top (reduced brackets)
- 2024: 4.82% flat (first year flat)
- 2025: 3.8% flat
- **2026: 3.8% flat** (accelerated via SF 2442, May 2024)
- Permanent statutory: 3.9% flat

### Starting point

IA begins with federal AGI with state-specific adjustments.

### Federal income tax deduction

IA historically allowed deduction for federal income tax paid; **REPEALED** effective with flat-tax transition (HF 2317). No longer available 2023+.

### Capital gains

Taxed at flat 3.8%. IA has historical capital gains exclusion for sale of qualified IA business / farm held 10+ years [VERIFY current status post-reform].

### Retirement income exemption (age 55+)

**Iowa Code §422.7 (as amended by HF 2317)**: All retirement income fully exempt for qualifying residents age 55+.

**Fully exempt sources**:
- Social Security
- Pension/annuity distributions (IPERS, private, federal, state, local)
- IRA distributions (traditional, Roth, SEP, SIMPLE)
- 401(k), 403(b), 457 distributions
- Military retirement
- Deferred compensation

**Under 55**: Standard IA tax applies (3.8% on all income).

**Priceless planning**: Iowa has become one of the best states for retirees — full retirement exemption + no estate/inheritance tax + flat 3.8% rate on any non-retirement income.

## PTET (Iowa Code §422.25C)

**Rate**: 3.8% for TY 2026 (tracks individual); reducing to 3.5% for TY 2027+ (per Tax Foundation notes on composite tax)
**Composite tax**: Required for nonresident members of pass-through entities on IA-source income
**Refundable credit**: Nonresident members receive refundable IA credit for composite tax paid
**Election**: Annual

## Corporate Income Tax

**Top rate**: 7.1% (reduced from 9.8% in 2022 reform)
**Lower tier**: 5.5% on income below threshold
**Transitioning**: Trigger-based reductions toward flat rate
**Base**: Federal taxable income with IA modifications

## Sales and use tax

**State rate**: 6%
**Local option**: up to 1% (about 98% of IA counties use the 1% local option)
**Combined effective**: 7% statewide in most areas

**Economic nexus**:
- $100,000 gross revenue (200-transaction threshold repealed)

## Property tax

**Effective rate**: 1.50% (above national average; a major IA weakness per Tax Foundation Competitiveness Index)
**No statewide cap**: Individual assessments and mill rates vary
**Homestead credit**: Modest reduction on primary residence
**Military exemption**: For veterans
**Disabled veterans**: Full exemption for 100% disabled

Property tax reform is the identified "next chapter" of IA tax reform per Tax Foundation analysis.

## Estate, gift, and inheritance tax

NONE.
- **Estate tax**: Eliminated effective 2022 (never imposed modern tax)
- **Inheritance tax**: Phased out from 2021 to 2025; **fully repealed effective January 1, 2025**
- **Gift tax**: Never imposed

Iowa is now fully transfer-tax-free at state level.

## Illinois reciprocity

IA has reciprocal agreement with Illinois only — Iowa residents working in IL pay only IA tax; IL residents working in IA pay only IL tax. Simplifies cross-border commuter compliance. No other reciprocal states.

## Priceless client considerations

### Retirees age 55+ relocating to Iowa
- Zero IA tax on any retirement income
- No estate/inheritance tax
- 3.8% flat on any earned/investment income
- Property tax moderate-to-high (1.50%)
- Growing attractiveness as retirement destination

### S corp / LLC owners
- PTET 3.8% captures federal SALT cap value
- Low rate means lower absolute savings than high-tax states
- Composite nonresident compliance required

### Real estate investors
- Higher property tax (1.50%)
- 3.8% on rental income
- IA capital gains exclusion for qualified holdings [VERIFY current status]

### Multi-state pass-through owners with IA operations
- IA PTET + composite required for nonresidents
- 3.8% rate competitive vs. other Midwest states
