# South Dakota (SD) — State Tax File

**The premier US trust jurisdiction**: South Dakota's primary relevance to Priceless is as a trust situs for irrevocable dynasty trusts, asset protection trusts, and DSTs for high-net-worth family planning. South Dakota holds over $500 billion in trust assets. The state's trust laws — combined with no state income tax — make it the most competitive trust jurisdiction in the United States and arguably globally.

For Priceless clients, SD engagement touchpoints are typically:
- **Dynasty trust situs** (multi-generational wealth transfer with no rule against perpetuities)
- **Domestic Asset Protection Trust (DAPT)** situs (2-year creditor limitation period)
- **Directed Trust** structures (family retains investment advisor control)
- Occasional resident clients (Sioux Falls, Rapid City)
- Multi-state business clients with SD operations

Very rare for Priceless to have active SD resident tax-planning engagements at the personal level (since there's no state income tax to plan around).

## Quick reference

- **Personal income tax**: NONE (constitutional voter approval required to impose)
- **Corporate income tax**: NONE
- **Bank Franchise Tax**: 6% on income of financial institutions (SDCL 10-44)
- **Sales tax**: 4.2% state base [VERIFY: rate was temporarily reduced from 4.5% to 4.2% for mid-2023 through mid-2027]; local additions up to 2%; combined max ~6.2%
- **Use tax**: same rate as sales tax
- **Contractor's Excise Tax**: 2% on prime contractors for realty improvement (unique SD feature)
- **Tourism Tax**: 1.5% additional on lodging, food service, admissions
- **Estate/inheritance/gift tax**: NONE
- **Franchise tax**: NONE (except Bank Franchise Tax)
- **Property tax**: local; average effective 1.14% (moderate)
- **PTET**: NOT APPLICABLE
- **Rule against perpetuities**: ABOLISHED (SDCL §43-5-8) — enables perpetual dynasty trusts

## Personal income tax (NONE)

South Dakota has no individual income tax. S.D. Const. Art. XI, §1 requires voter approval for any new general state tax — creating a practical constitutional barrier to introducing an income tax. The legislature cannot impose income tax without a referendum.

**What SD residents pay**:
- Federal income tax
- Federal FICA/SE tax
- SD sales tax on purchases (4.5% state + local)
- SD property tax (local only)
- Contractor's Excise Tax if in qualifying industry

**What SD residents DO NOT pay**:
- State income tax on wages, investments, capital gains, retirement distributions, trust distributions
- State tax on Social Security, pensions, IRA/401(k) distributions

### Residency establishment

SD residency established via typical factors:
- SD driver's license
- Voter registration
- Vehicle registration
- SD address for key accounts
- Physical presence

No statutory day-count for state tax purposes (since no state income tax exists). Documentation supports departing-state residency audits.

**SD as retirement destination**: Low property tax, no state income tax, lower cost of living. Some Priceless retiree clients consider SD as alternative to FL/TX.

## South Dakota Trust Jurisdiction (CRITICAL — Priceless Full-Wealth)

South Dakota is the preeminent US trust jurisdiction. Key features:

### Abolition of Rule Against Perpetuities (SDCL §43-5-8)

**What it means**: SD has completely abolished the common-law rule against perpetuities. Trusts can exist for **unlimited duration** — effectively forever.

**Comparison**:
- Delaware: 110 years (limited by statute)
- Nevada: 365 years
- Alaska: 1,000 years
- Wyoming: 1,000 years
- **South Dakota: UNLIMITED** — no time limit

**Practical value**: For multi-generational wealth transfer, SD dynasty trusts can hold assets for dozens of generations without termination. Estate tax paid once at settlement of trust; never again regardless of generations.

### Directed Trust structure (SDCL §55-2-15)

Separates trustee functions into:
- **Administrative Trustee**: SD bank/trust company (fiduciary duty limited to administrative functions)
- **Investment Trust Advisor**: Family member, investment advisor, or firm (directs investment decisions)
- **Distribution Advisor**: Family member or independent party (directs distributions)

Benefits:
- Family retains investment control while getting SD trust benefits
- Reduced trust company liability (limited to administration)
- Lower trustee fees (administrative-only roles)
- Flexibility for complex investment holdings (private businesses, real estate, alternative assets)

### Domestic Asset Protection Trust (SDCL §55-16) — "Qualified Disposition in Trust Act"

**Self-settled spendthrift trust**: Settlor can be discretionary beneficiary and still achieve creditor protection
**Limitation period**: 2 years for most creditors (except pre-existing claims)
**Standard of proof**: clear and convincing evidence of fraudulent transfer required
**Comparison**: Nevada NSSPT has similar 2-year period; SD is well-established with broad case law

### Trust Privacy (SDCL §55-16-9)

- **No court registration** required for private trusts
- **No public filing** of trust documents
- Trust existence, terms, and beneficiaries are confidential
- Court proceedings involving trusts can be sealed

**Compared to other states**: Many states require trust filings that become public records via probate or other court proceedings; SD maintains full privacy.

### No state income tax on trust income

Trust income accumulated in a SD trust is not subject to state income tax. For irrevocable trusts holding substantial investment portfolios, this creates significant multi-year compounding advantage.

**Example**: $50M irrevocable trust earning 7% = $3.5M annual income
- If trust is resident in CA (13.3% state rate): $465K/year state tax drag
- If trust is resident in SD (0%): full $3.5M compounds tax-deferred (federal still applies)

### Trust company ecosystem

Major SD trust companies:
- First Western Trust Bank (Rapid City)
- Black Hills Trust (Rapid City)
- South Dakota Trust Company (Sioux Falls)
- Bankers Trust (Sioux Falls)

**Pricing**: SD trust company fees typically 15-50 basis points of assets under administration, with family-specific negotiation. Higher than retail wealth management but significantly lower than DE/NY big-bank trust departments.

For Priceless Full-Wealth clients considering trust situs:

## Business taxes

### NO corporate income tax

South Dakota has no corporate income tax. C corporations, S corporations, LLCs, partnerships pay no state income tax on business income.

### Bank Franchise Tax (SDCL 10-44) — narrow exception

**Applies to**: Financial institutions (banks, savings & loans, trust companies) operating in SD
**Rate**: 6% of "earnings based on Federal taxable income" (with SD modifications)
**Minimum tax**: $200 per year
**Does NOT apply to**: general business corporations, LLCs, partnerships

This tax is highly industry-specific and does not affect typical Priceless clients unless they own a SD financial institution (not common).

### Sales and use tax (SDCL Chapter 10-45)

**State rate**: 4.2% temporarily [VERIFY: reduced from 4.5% in 2023 legislation through June 30, 2027; will revert to 4.5% absent extension]
**Local rates (municipalities)**: 0-2% additional
**Maximum combined**: ~6.2% (example: Sioux Falls 6.2%, Rapid City 6.2%, small municipalities often 4.2% state-only)

**Nexus thresholds (post-Wayfair; SD was the Wayfair case plaintiff)**:
- $100,000 in SD gross sales OR 200 transactions in prior or current year (the original Wayfair thresholds)
- Marketplace facilitator collection required

**Streamlined Sales Tax member**: SD simplifies multi-state seller compliance

**Services taxed**: SD broadly taxes services (unlike most states); professional services, personal care, repair, etc.

**Services NOT taxed**: legal services, medical services, educational, agricultural inputs, most grocery food items

### Contractor's Excise Tax (SDCL 10-46B)

**Unique SD feature**: 2% tax on prime contractors engaged in realty improvement
**Base**: Total contract price including labor and materials
**Who pays**: Prime contractor (as defined by 1987 Standard Industrial Classification)
**Subcontractors**: Generally exempt if prime paid the tax

This is effectively an additional sales tax on construction activity; unique to SD (most states tax construction materials via sales tax on purchase, not on contract value).

### Tourism tax (SDCL 10-46A)

**Rate**: 1.5% additional on lodging, food service, admissions, amusements
**Combined with sales tax**: lodging in Sioux Falls ~7.7% (4.2% state + 2% local + 1.5% tourism)

**Rate range**: 0.0% to 9.35% (experienced rates)
**New employer**: 1.2%

## Post-OBBBA conformity matrix

N/A at individual and general corporate level — no state income tax.

Bank Franchise Tax (limited to financial institutions) conforms to federal taxable income with SD modifications:

| Federal provision (OBBBA) | SD Bank Franchise Tax treatment |
|---|---|
| 100% bonus depreciation | Conforms to federal (SD uses federal taxable income as starting point) |
| §163(j) EBITDA post-OBBBA | Conforms |
| §174 R&D | Conforms |

For non-financial-institution clients: OBBBA conformity is irrelevant at SD state level.

## Property tax

**Administered at county level**; SD does not levy state property tax.

**Effective rates**:
- Statewide average: 1.14%
- Sioux Falls: ~1.40%
- Rapid City: ~1.30%
- Rural counties: 0.8-1.2%

**Owner-occupied primary residence reduction**: Approximately 50% of locally-determined tax rate [VERIFY mechanism via county]

**Freeze for seniors**: Age 65+ low-income seniors can freeze assessed value

## Estate, gift, and inheritance tax

NONE at state level. South Dakota has no estate tax, no inheritance tax, no gift tax.

Combined with SD's trust laws, this makes SD ideal for wealth preservation.

Federal estate tax applies at $15M (post-OBBBA permanent). SD residents face federal-only estate tax burden.

## Priceless client considerations

### Dynasty trust clients (Full-Wealth tier)
- SD #1 dynasty trust jurisdiction
- Unlimited duration
- No state income tax
- Strong privacy
- Directed trust structure
- Priceless refers to estate attorney for drafting; can suggest trustees; handles tax preparation for trust

### DAPT (asset protection) clients
- 2-year statute of limitations for creditor claims (SDCL §55-16)
- Clear and convincing evidence standard
- SD-based trustee required
- Works for professionals (doctors, attorneys) concerned about liability
- Must be set up BEFORE creditor claims arise; fraudulent transfer analysis

### Retirees relocating to SD
- No state income tax; low property tax; moderate cost of living
- Less common than FL relocation but competitive option
- Climate consideration (cold winters)
- Sioux Falls has growing medical and financial services sectors

### S corp owners resident in SD
- No state income tax
- No state S corp tax at entity level
- Standard business license; sales tax if applicable
- Contractor's Excise Tax if in construction

### Construction industry
- Contractor's Excise Tax 2% on prime contractor
- Significant impact on SD construction pricing and compliance

### E-commerce with SD customers
- SD was the Wayfair case plaintiff
- $100K / 200 transactions economic nexus (original Wayfair thresholds)
- Marketplace facilitator collection

## Multi-state mechanics

### SD resident with out-of-state income
- No SD tax to credit against (no SD income tax)
- Other-state returns filed normally
- No double taxation issue from SD side

### Non-resident with SD-source income
- Individuals: no SD tax
- Corporations: no SD income tax (except Bank Franchise Tax for financial institutions)
- Sales tax if nexus

### Out-of-state client with SD trust situs
- Trust sitused in SD; administrative trustee in SD
- Investment decisions can be family-directed via directed trust structure
- Federal tax still applies at trust or beneficiary level
- **Other state's tax**: Some states assert jurisdiction over trusts with in-state beneficiaries (e.g., CA has aggressive position); coordinate with SD trust attorney on defensible structure to avoid non-SD state tax on trust income

### NING trust (Nevada or Delaware INING) situs in SD
- "South Dakota Incomplete-Gift Non-Grantor Trust" variants
- Complex structure for high-tax-state residents to achieve state income tax deferral on investment income
- Heavily scrutinized by CA (which issued regulations specifically targeting NING-like structures)
- Requires careful drafting and operation; partner review

## Key dates for 2026 engagements

No state income tax filing dates for individuals. Key dates for SD business/trust engagements:
- **Monthly/quarterly**: Sales tax returns (per filing frequency assigned by SDDOR)
- **Bank Franchise Tax**: Due 15 days after federal return due date (April 30 for calendar year)
- **Annual report for corporations/LLCs**: First day of anniversary month; $50 annual report fee (corporations), $50 (LLCs)
- **Trust fiduciary returns**: Federal Form 1041 at federal schedule; no SD state return
