# South Carolina (SC) — State Tax File

South Carolina enacted temporary rate reduction via **FY 2026 Budget Act** (June 2025): top rate reduced from 6.2% to **6.0% from July 1, 2025 through June 30, 2026** — effectively making 2026 calendar year 6.0% for most filers (though the fiscal-year mismatch creates mid-year application nuances).

SC offers significant retirement exemptions making it popular for retiree relocations. **Active Trade or Business Income** at flat 3% rate is a unique SC feature (via § 12-6-545).

Low Priceless concentration. Some Charleston/Greenville clients; meaningful retiree migration from NE/Midwest.

## Quick reference

- **Personal income tax**: Top rate **6.0% (temporary; 7/1/2025 - 6/30/2026)**; reverts to 6.2% after 6/30/2026 unless extended
- **Active Trade or Business Income**: Flat **3%** (§ 12-6-545) — SC-unique benefit for pass-through business income
- **Corporate income tax**: 5% flat
- **PTET**: Yes (§ 12-6-3776)
- **Sales tax**: 6% state + local up to 3% (combined max 9%); groceries exempt from state (subject to 1-2% local)
- **Estate tax**: None
- **Inheritance tax**: None
- **Social Security**: Fully exempt (not included in SC taxable income)
- **Retirement income deduction**: Up to $15,000 per taxpayer (age 65+)
- **Military retirement**: Up to $17,500 deduction (under age 65) / up to $30,000 (age 65+); varies
- **Standard deduction**: Conforms federal ($16,100 single / $32,200 MFJ 2026)
- **Filing deadline**: April 15 (conforms federal)

## Personal income tax

### Rate structure

SC simplified rates in recent years. Current top rate **6.0% (effective 7/1/2025 - 6/30/2026)**. Prior to 2022 reform, SC had 6 brackets; now effectively collapsed to:
- Lower rate tiers (small income only)
- **Top rate 6.0%** applies to nearly all income above modest threshold

### Active Trade or Business Income (ATBI) — § 12-6-545

**Key SC benefit**: Income from "active trade or business" sources — typically S corp distributions and active partnership/LLC income — is taxed at flat **3%** instead of 6.0% top rate.

**Qualifications**:
- Material participation required (similar to federal passive activity rules)
- Active trade or business (not passive rental or investment)
- Pass-through income to SC resident owners

**Worked example**: SC resident with $400K S corp income (active):
- If treated as ATBI: $400K × 3% = $12,000 SC tax
- If treated as ordinary: $400K × 6.0% = $24,000 SC tax
- **ATBI savings: $12,000/year** — very material

This makes SC highly favorable for active pass-through business owners.

### Retirement income

- **Social Security**: Fully exempt
- **Retirement income deduction**: Up to $15,000 per taxpayer (age 65+); includes IRAs, pensions
- **Under age 65**: Up to $3,000 retirement deduction
- **Military retirement**: Up to $17,500 (under 65) / up to $30,000 (age 65+)

SC is retirement-friendly. For MFJ both 65+: $30,000 retirement exemption + unlimited Social Security.

## Pass-Through Entity Tax (PTET)

- **Rate**: 6.0% top or 3% ATBI (tracks active/non-active character)
- **Election**: Annual
- **Credit**: Refundable

Combined with ATBI benefit, SC PTET is uniquely structured — owners get both the federal SALT cap workaround AND the 3% ATBI rate.

## Corporate income tax

- **Rate**: 5% flat
- **Base**: Federal taxable income with SC modifications
- **Apportionment**: Single sales factor

## Filing mechanics

- **Deadline**: April 15 (conforms federal)
- **Forms**: SC 1040 (resident); SC 1040 Schedule NR (nonresident)

## Priceless client scenarios

### SC S corp owner (ATBI benefit)
**Setup**: Charleston resident; S corp with $600K active business income.

**SC tax**:
- ATBI at 3%: $600K × 3% = $18,000
- Alternative (if not ATBI-qualified): $600K × 6.0% = $36,000

**ATBI savings**: $18,000/year (50% reduction). Priceless must carefully document material participation.

### SC retiree (popular relocation)
**Setup**: MFJ both 67; $60K combined pension + $40K Social Security.

**SC tax**:
- Social Security: Fully exempt ($40K)
- Pension deduction: $15,000 × 2 = $30,000
- Taxable: $30,000
- Less standard deduction: $32,200
- Net taxable: $0
- **SC tax: $0**

SC is highly favorable for retirees. Combined with no estate tax and low cost of living, major migration destination.
