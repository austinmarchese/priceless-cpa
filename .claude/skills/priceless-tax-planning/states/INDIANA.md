# Indiana (IN) — State Tax File

**Low flat-rate state with continuing reductions**: Indiana's flat rate dropped to 2.95% effective 1/1/2026 (per HB 1001, 2023). Further reduction to 2.9% scheduled 1/1/2027. SB 451 (April 2025) provides for continuing reductions to 2.55% in 0.05% increments from 2030 onward if revenue triggers are met.

Small Priceless presence; Indianapolis/Carmel professionals and some multi-state clients.

## Quick reference

- **Personal income tax**: 2.95% flat (2026); 2.9% scheduled (2027)
- **Corporate income tax**: 4.9% flat
- **Local Income Tax (LIT)**: County-specific; range 0.25% to 3.38% (Indiana unique: all counties impose LIT at varying rates)
- **PTET**: Yes (Ind. Code §6-3-2.1); elective; tracks personal rate
- **Sales tax**: 7% state (no local supplement — 7% uniform statewide)
- **Estate/inheritance/gift tax**: NONE (inheritance tax repealed 2013)
- **Property tax**: effective 0.82% (moderate)
- **1% property tax cap (state constitutional)**: Limits residential property tax to 1% of gross assessed value

## Personal income tax

**Rate trajectory**:
- 2022: 3.23%
- 2023: 3.15%
- 2024: 3.05%
- 2025: 3.00%
- **2026: 2.95%**
- 2027 scheduled: 2.9%
- 2030+ (if triggers met): phased down to 2.55% by 0.05% increments

### Starting point

IN begins with federal AGI, applies IN-specific add-backs and deductions. Federal bonus depreciation typically added back.

### Capital gains

Taxed at flat 2.95% (ordinary income treatment).

### Retirement income

- Social Security: fully exempt
- Military retirement: fully exempt (deduction)
- Federal civil service: $16,000 age-based exemption
- State/local government retirement: partial
- IRA/401(k): taxable at 2.95%

## Local Income Tax (LIT) — Critical Indiana Feature

**Authority**: Ind. Code §6-3.6

**ALL 92 Indiana counties impose LIT** — Indiana is unique in having universal county income tax.

**Rate range**: 0.25% (lowest) to 3.38% (highest — Pulaski County)
**Major counties**:
- Marion County (Indianapolis): 2.02%
- Hamilton County (Carmel): 1.10%
- Allen County (Fort Wayne): 1.48%
- Lake County (Gary): 1.50%
- St. Joseph County (South Bend): 1.75%

**Effective combined rate for high-earner**: 2.95% state + 2%+ local = 4.95%+ total state-local

**LIT applies to**: Residents of a county (taxed on all income) and some nonresidents working in the county

**Priceless planning**: County of residence matters. Moving from Marion to Hamilton County saves ~0.92% on all income. For $500K earner, that's $4,600/year.

## PTET (Ind. Code §6-3-2.1)

**Status**: Elective
**Rate**: 2.95% (matches personal rate; **does NOT include LIT** — owners still owe LIT separately)
**Eligible**: S corps, partnerships, LLCs taxed as partnerships
**Federal SALT cap workaround**: Yes — but note that LIT paid by owner individually is still subject to SALT cap

**Planning nuance**: IN PTET covers state tax only; owners still pay county LIT on K-1 income, which is still subject to federal SALT cap.

## Corporate Income Tax

**Rate**: 4.9% flat (effective July 2021)
**Apportionment**: Single sales factor (complete 2019)

## Sales and use tax

**State rate**: 7% (one of higher states with no local supplement)
**No local sales tax**: IN does not permit local sales tax; uniform 7% statewide
**Food**: exempt from sales tax
**Prepared food**: 9% combined (7% state + 2% food and beverage tax in some counties)

**Economic nexus**:
- $100,000 gross revenue OR 200 transactions

## Property tax

**Effective rate**: 0.82%
**Constitutional 1% cap (residential)**: Indiana Const. Art. X, §1 — residential property tax cannot exceed 1% of gross assessed value
**Rental/commercial caps**: 2% and 3%
**Homestead deduction**: Lesser of $48,000 or 60% of assessed value
**Over-65 property tax freeze**: Available for eligible seniors

## Estate, gift, inheritance

NONE. Indiana inheritance tax repealed effective 2013 (was last state to have both estate and inheritance taxes; both now gone).

## Priceless client considerations

### Hamilton County (Carmel, Fishers, Westfield) residents
- Low LIT (1.10%) vs. Marion (2.02%)
- Moderate housing costs
- Popular Indianapolis suburb

### Marion County (Indianapolis) residents
- Higher LIT (2.02%)
- Urban professional employment
- Consider LIT in total burden

### S corp / LLC owners
- PTET captures SALT cap federal value on state tax only
- LIT paid separately at personal level; still subject to SALT cap
- Standard compliance

### Retirees in IN
- Social Security and military retirement fully exempt
- Moderate state income tax
- Low property tax on primary residence (1% cap)
- No estate/inheritance tax
