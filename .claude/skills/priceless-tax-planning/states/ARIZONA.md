# Arizona (AZ) — State Tax File

**Lowest graduated-converted rate in US**: Arizona's 2.5% flat individual income tax rate is the lowest among the 41 states with income tax. Achieved via 2021 reform that accelerated phase-in to 2022/2023. Property tax moderate; sales tax (Transaction Privilege Tax) relatively high when combined with local.

**Common Priceless destination** for CA departees (Phoenix metro growth; semiconductors Intel/TSMC; lifestyle). Lifestyle trade-offs: summer heat (110°F+); water scarcity concerns; but significant tax savings vs. CA.

## Quick reference

- **Personal income tax**: 2.5% flat (since 2023; post-2021 reform)
- **Corporate income tax**: 4.9% flat
- **PTET**: Yes (A.R.S. §43-1126); 2.5% flat at entity level
- **Transaction Privilege Tax (TPT/sales tax)**: 5.6% state + up to 5.6% local (combined max 11.2% in some cities)
- **Standard deduction (2025)**: $14,600 single / $29,200 MFJ (federal-linked)
- **Dependent tax credit**: $100/dependent (under 17) / $25 (17+); phases out above FAGI $200K single / $400K MFJ
- **Charitable itemized bonus**: 34% add-on to standard deduction for those who would have itemized charitable
- **Estate/inheritance/gift tax**: NONE
- **Property tax**: effective 0.63% (moderate; below national average)
- **Social Security**: 100% exempt from state tax
- **Military retired pay**: 100% exempt
- **Retirement income**: pensions/401(k)/IRA taxable at 2.5%

## Personal income tax

### Rate structure

**2.5% flat** on Arizona taxable income. No brackets. Same rate all income levels.

**Historical context**:
- Pre-2021: Graduated rates 2.59% to 4.5%
- 2022: Moved to 2.55%/2.98% two-bracket
- 2023+: Single flat 2.5%

### Starting point: federal AGI

AZ uses federal AGI as starting point, with AZ additions and subtractions:

**Common AZ subtractions**:
- Social Security benefits (100%)
- US government bond interest
- AZ public pension exclusions (partial)
- 529 plan contributions
- Charitable tax credit contributions
- Military retirement (100%)
- Federal QSBS conformity

**Common AZ additions**:
- State municipal bond interest from non-AZ states
- Federal bonus depreciation (AZ disconformity)

### Standard deduction

Follows federal: $15,000 single / $30,000 MFJ (2025 inflation-adjusted); $15,750/$31,500 for 2026 [VERIFY].

**Charitable bonus**: AZ allows a 34% enhancement to standard deduction equal to 34% of what the taxpayer could have claimed as itemized charitable deduction. Unique AZ feature encouraging charitable giving at higher levels.

### Dependent tax credit

- $100 per dependent under 17
- $25 per dependent 17+
- Phase-out: begins at FAGI $200K single / $400K MFJ

### Capital gains

Taxed as ordinary income at flat 2.5%. No preferential rate. Lower than most states; notably lower than CA (13.3%) and NY (10.9% state + 3.876% NYC).

## PTET — Pass-Through Entity Tax (A.R.S. §43-1126)

**Rate**: 2.5% flat (tracks individual rate exactly)
**Election**: Annual; must elect each year
**Eligible**: S corps, partnerships, LLCs taxed as partnerships
**Owner credit**: Refundable credit on AZ personal return
**Federal SALT deductible**: Yes (Notice 2020-75 safe harbor)

**Planning note**: Since AZ PTET rate (2.5%) matches individual rate, election is tax-neutral at AZ level — entire value comes from federal SALT cap workaround. For AZ residents whose SALT cap binds, PTET election captures federal deduction at 37% federal rate × AZ tax paid.

**Example**: S corp owner with $500K K-1 income:
- Without PTET: $500K × 2.5% = $12.5K AZ tax; no federal deduction (SALT cap)
- With PTET: $500K × 2.5% = $12.5K entity tax; federal deduction $12.5K × 37% = $4,625 federal savings; owner gets $12.5K AZ credit

**PTET election form**: Form 140PTE; payment required by PTE return due date

## Corporate income tax

**Rate**: 4.9% flat
**Base**: Federal taxable income with AZ adjustments
**Filing**: Form 120 (corporate); Form 120S (S corp informational)
**Apportionment**: Single sales factor (election available; alternative three-factor with double-weighted sales)

## Transaction Privilege Tax (TPT)

Arizona's sales tax-like levy (technically a "transaction privilege" tax on the seller for the privilege of doing business; in practice passed to consumer).

**State rate**: 5.6%
**Local rates (city + county)**: 0% to ~5.6% additional
**Combined max**: 11.2% (some Tucson-area jurisdictions)

**Major cities**:
- Phoenix: 8.6% combined
- Scottsdale: 8.6% combined
- Tucson: 8.7% combined
- Tempe: 8.1% combined
- Mesa: 8.3% combined

**Economic nexus (post-Wayfair)**:
- $100,000 in AZ sales in prior calendar year
- Marketplace facilitator collection required
- AZ is a SST non-member (complex multi-city local rates; software helps)

**What's taxed**:
- Tangible personal property
- Certain services
- Contracting (prime contractor; 65% of gross receipts)
- Hotel/lodging
- Restaurants

## Property tax

**Administered at county level**; state does not levy general property tax.

**Effective rates**:
- Statewide: 0.63% (below national average)
- Maricopa County (Phoenix): 0.66%
- Pima County (Tucson): 0.86%
- Rural counties: 0.4-1.0%

**Assessment ratio**: 10% for primary residences; 18% for commercial; 16% for agriculture
**Senior property tax freeze**: Age 65+ with income below ~$41K (2026 threshold)
**Primary residence classification**: Reduced assessment ratio vs. secondary homes

## Estate, gift, and inheritance tax

NONE at state level.

## Residency

- Physical presence or domicile factors
- AZ Form 140 for full-year residents
- Form 140PY for part-year; Form 140NR for nonresidents

**For CA departees to AZ**: FTB will audit aggressively; AZ driver's license + voter registration + vehicle registration needed as baseline documentation. See `/states/CALIFORNIA.md` for departure mechanics.

## Priceless client considerations

### CA departees to AZ
- Primary relocation pattern
- At $200K AGI: save ~$20K/year vs. CA (13.3% top vs. 2.5%)
- FTB residency audit still applies if CA exit
- Document AZ physical presence + life transition
- Consider 6-month waiting period before CA asset sale (QSBS, RSUs vesting, business sale)

### S corporation owners in AZ
- Entity-level AZ tax at 2.5% regardless of PTET
- PTET election captures federal SALT cap workaround value
- $500K K-1 owner: PTET saves ~$4,625 federal (37% of $12.5K AZ tax)
- Low burden state overall; AZ S corp very friendly

### Real estate investors
- No AZ income tax on rental income beyond 2.5% flat
- Moderate property tax (0.63% effective)
- No state transfer tax
- Single sales factor apportionment (for entities)

### Retirees
- Social Security 100% exempt; military retirement 100% exempt
- Pensions/401(k)/IRA taxed at 2.5% (low)
- Property tax freeze age 65+ (if income limit met)
- Moderate property taxes; high sales tax burden on consumption
- Popular retirement destination

### Doctors and professionals
- AZ has favorable tax environment for professional income
- 2.5% flat on all income including professional fees
- PLLCs/PCs subject to same PTET rules
- Phoenix/Scottsdale/Tucson major markets

## Multi-state mechanics

### AZ resident with out-of-state income
- AZ allows credit for taxes paid to other states (A.R.S. §43-1071)
- File Form 140 with Schedule CR

### Non-resident with AZ income
- File Form 140NR
- AZ tax on AZ-source income only
