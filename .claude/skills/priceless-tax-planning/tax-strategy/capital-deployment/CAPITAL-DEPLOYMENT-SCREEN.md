# Capital Deployment Screen

## Purpose

Priceless's 13 capital deployment strategies span aircraft, oil/gas, equipment leasing, QOZ, DAF, CRT, CLT, private foundation, §1031 advanced, §1202 QSBS (in strategies folder), foreign/GILTI, movie tax credits, and promoter patterns (defense file, not a strategy).

Within any given client situation, 2-3 of these are relevant and 10+ are not. This screen quickly identifies the relevant 2-3 so partner time is spent on real opportunities rather than exhaustive walkthroughs.

## Prerequisites (gate checks)

Before running this screen, confirm:

- [ ] Client AGI > $750K (from CAPITAL-DEPLOYMENT-FRAMEWORK.md gate 1)
- [ ] Operators 1-7 optimized (core strategies exhausted) — not skipping ahead
- [ ] Client has deployable capital (liquidity available for deployment)
- [ ] Client time horizon > 1 year (most deployment strategies require multi-year commitment)
- [ ] Client risk tolerance matches deployment category (audit exposure, illiquidity, execution complexity)
- [ ] Client engagement letter covers advisory scope (capital deployment advice is coordination, not execution)

If any gate fails, DO NOT run this screen. Return to core engagement planning.

## Step 2: Strategy relevance matrix

For each capital deployment strategy, determine relevance based on client profile. Score each as:

- **Primary** (top-3 for this client; explore in depth)
- **Possible** (could apply; secondary consideration)
- **Not applicable** (clear no-match; document reason and move on)

### QOZ-FUNDS.md (Qualified Opportunity Zone)

**Primary when**:
- Client has large capital gain in current year ($500K+) needing deferral
- 10-year investment horizon acceptable
- Client wants real estate / business operating exposure (not passive income)

**Possible when**:
- Client has moderate capital gain ($100K-$500K) and tax-deferral value exceeds fund fees
- Client already holds capital gain positions considering harvest

**Not applicable when**:
- Client has no capital gains to defer
- Client cannot commit 10-year illiquidity
- California resident (significant non-conformity limits benefit)
- Client needs current cash flow

**Post-OBBBA note**: QOZ is now permanent; 10-year appreciation exclusion preserved; but December 31, 2031 deferral trigger deadline applies to post-OBBBA investments.

### DAF-ADVANCED.md (Donor-Advised Fund, multi-generational)

**Primary when**:
- Client has significant appreciated assets (stock, business interests, real estate)
- Charitable intent (not just tax optimization)
- High current-year AGI with bunching benefit >$100K
- Multi-year charitable giving trajectory

**Possible when**:
- Client expecting large liquidity event (business sale, IPO) and wants current deduction
- Client has pre-exit QSBS positions (pre-exit gifting strategy)
- CRT + DAF integrated structure contemplated

**Not applicable when**:
- Client has no charitable intent (DAF requires giving eventually)
- Client lacks appreciated assets
- Post-OBBBA: Consider 0.5% AGI charitable floor effect; DAF still valuable but marginal benefit reduced
- Post-OBBBA: 35% charitable deduction cap (vs. pre-OBBBA 37% marginal) for highest-bracket filers

### SECTION-1031-ADVANCED.md (Reverse exchanges, improvement exchanges, DSTs)

**Primary when**:
- Client has rental / investment real estate considering sale
- Cannot complete simultaneous like-kind exchange (timing mismatch)
- Wants to consolidate or diversify real estate portfolio
- Replacement property not identified at sale

**Possible when**:
- Client has real estate with large appreciation but specific property locked up
- Client considering DST for passive real estate exposure

**Not applicable when**:
- Client holds only primary residence (not eligible; §121 governs instead)
- Client wants to exit real estate entirely (cash out)
- State decoupling significant (post-TCJA federal-only §1031)

### EQUIPMENT-LEASING-FUNDS.md (§469(c)(6) categorical passive)

**Primary when**:
- Client has passive income (K-1 passive, rental) generating losses that can't be currently used
- Client needs passive activity income to unlock passive losses elsewhere
- AGI supports meaningful position ($200K-$500K+ invested typical)
- Client comfortable with single-tenant commercial lease exposure

**Possible when**:
- Client has §469 grouping strategy being implemented
- Client wants 100% bonus depreciation (permanent post-OBBBA)

**Not applicable when**:
- Client has no passive income needing offset
- Client wants cash flow (not deferred depreciation)
- California resident (state decouples from bonus depreciation)

### AIRCRAFT.md

**Primary when**:
- Client has legitimate business travel need (not primary personal use)
- Business use >50% documented (§280F threshold)
- Partner always required to review before recommending
- Very high AGI ($3M+) where tax deferral matters

**Possible when**:
- Client considering business-owned aircraft for legitimate operations
- Fractional ownership model (NetJets, Flexjet, etc.)

**Not applicable when**:
- Personal use dominates (>50% personal fails §280F)
- "Tail wagging dog" test fails (tax tail ≠ business dog)
- Client has not operated business aircraft historically (elevated audit risk)

**Audit profile**: HIGH — Priceless requires partner signoff on every aircraft strategy.

### OIL-GAS-WORKING-INTERESTS.md (§469(c)(3) active exception)

**Primary when**:
- Client has significant AGI and wants active tax treatment (not passive)
- Client comfortable with oil/gas exposure and illiquidity
- Client has advisor or operator relationship (specialist engagement separate)
- Intangible Drilling Costs (IDC) deduction valuable (>$100K typical)

**Possible when**:
- Client has domestic oil/gas investment access through established operator
- Texas, Oklahoma, Louisiana operations (state-specific tax benefits)
- Percentage depletion valuable over cost depletion

**Not applicable when**:
- Client seeks income (oil/gas is deferred-return investment)
- Client cannot tolerate commodity price volatility
- Client is investor-only without working interest (passive; different treatment)

### CRT-CRUT.md (Charitable Remainder Trust / Unitrust)

**Primary when**:
- Client has highly appreciated asset (stock, real estate, business interest)
- Client wants income stream AND charitable deduction
- Client wants to diversify appreciated position without immediate gain
- 10% minimum remainder test can be met

**Possible when**:
- Client integrating CRT with DAF (covered in DAF-ADVANCED.md)
- Client has §1361 S corp issue (CRT cannot hold S corp stock)

**Not applicable when**:
- Client wants full control of corpus (CRT is irrevocable)
- Remainder to charity < 10% of contribution (fails test)
- Client has no charitable intent

### PRIVATE-FOUNDATION.md

**Primary when**:
- Client's charitable giving > $5M annually OR total charitable wealth > $20M
- Multi-generational philanthropic program desired
- Family employment in foundation (subject to §4946 disqualified person rules)

**Possible when**:
- Client considering DAF-to-PF conversion at scale
- Client running scholarship or specific-purpose charitable programs

**Not applicable when**:
- Total giving < $2M (DAF is better structure)
- Client wants simplicity (PF compliance is significant)
- Client cannot maintain §4942 5% minimum distribution requirement

### CLT.md (Charitable Lead Trust)

**Primary when**:
- Client has wealth transfer goals (pass to heirs efficiently)
- Current §7520 rate favorable (low rate = higher remainder value to heirs)
- Client has appreciated assets with long-term growth expectation
- Grantor trust status desired for current deduction OR non-grantor for estate freeze

**Possible when**:
- Client implementing zeroed-out CLAT (maximum wealth transfer)
- Client's current charitable giving pattern already structured

**Not applicable when**:
- Client has no substantial wealth transfer need
- §7520 rate high (makes structure less favorable)
- Client wants current access to assets

### FOREIGN-GILTI.md (§951A, §250, §962 election)

**Primary when**:
- Client has foreign corporate holdings or CFC interests
- Form 5471 filing requirements exist
- §962 election benefits exceed costs (individual taxed as corporation on GILTI)
- Puerto Rico Act 60 is SEPARATELY covered by Priceless PR Advisors (not this file)

**Possible when**:
- Client planning foreign investment structure
- Non-US business with US owner

**Not applicable when**:
- Client has no foreign income
- Foreign holdings are passive investment (PFIC, not CFC; different rules)

### MOVIE-TAX-CREDITS.md (state film credit purchase)

**Primary when**:
- Client owes significant state income tax (target state credit)
- Client has cash available for credit purchase
- Target states: GA, LA, IL, NY (established credit markets)
- Post-tax yield vs. investment alternative is favorable

**Possible when**:
- Client's business generates substantial state tax liability
- Buyer-side only; not §181 investment partnership (different file, not covered)

**Not applicable when**:
- Client has low state tax liability
- Client lives in no-income-tax state (FL, TX, WY, etc.)
- Credit discount available < 10% (marginal benefit)

### PROMOTER-PATTERNS-PARTNER-DEFENSE.md (defense file)

**Not a strategy** — this is the framework for evaluating strategies CLIENTS bring in. Use when:

- Client mentions FX trading partnership, §181 movie shelter, conservation easement, aggressive cost seg, syndicated §831(b) captive, IUL with tax optimization claims
- Outside promoter, marketer, or "tax strategist" is selling a structure

Always run through this file BEFORE validating a client-brought strategy. Most fail the framework.

## Step 3: Scoring and prioritization

After marking each strategy as Primary / Possible / Not applicable, apply these filters:

### Narrow to top 3 Primary strategies

If more than 3 strategies scored Primary, apply these tiebreakers in order:
1. **Current-year tax urgency** (TY 2025 position must be taken this year)
2. **Audit profile preference** (client tolerance for aggressive positions)
3. **Execution complexity** (client tolerance for coordination load)
4. **Advisor relationship fit** (do we have trusted specialists for this strategy)
5. **Integration value** (strategies that stack with client's existing positions)

### Specific client profile → typical strategy set

Pattern-matching for common client profiles:

**E-commerce founder post-exit ($10M+ liquidity event)**
→ Primary: §1202 QSBS (from strategies) + DAF-ADVANCED + QOZ
→ Possible: CRT-CRUT, PRIVATE-FOUNDATION
→ Reference: `industries/E-COMMERCE.md`, `strategies/QSBS-SECTION-1202.md`

**Real estate owner with REPS / large rental portfolio**
→ Primary: SECTION-1031-ADVANCED (reverse/improvement) + EQUIPMENT-LEASING-FUNDS (passive income shelter)
→ Possible: COST-SEGREGATION (from strategies), DAF-ADVANCED
→ Reference: `industries/REAL-ESTATE-OWNER.md`

**Software / AI founder pre-exit (large unrealized QSBS position)**
→ Primary: QSBS stacking + pre-exit DAF-ADVANCED gifting + future QOZ post-sale
→ Possible: CRT-CRUT at exit, §1202 pre-gift to children
→ Reference: `industries/SOFTWARE-AI.md`, `strategies/QSBS-SECTION-1202.md`

**Medical practice owner (SSTB) with DB Plan + high income**
→ Primary: DB Plan (from strategies) + DAF-ADVANCED + QOZ for capital gains
→ Possible: EQUIPMENT-LEASING-FUNDS (if passive income), AIRCRAFT (rarely; partner review)
→ Reference: `industries/DOCTORS-MEDICAL.md`

**RIA / investment firm with scale**
→ Primary: §199A optimization (from strategies; SSTB phase-out workarounds) + DAF-ADVANCED + CLT for wealth transfer
→ Possible: PRIVATE-FOUNDATION (if giving >$5M), §1202 for portfolio companies
→ Reference: `industries/INVESTMENT-FIRMS.md`

**Business exit-stage client (selling operating business)**
→ Primary: Installment sale structure + QOZ + §1202 if eligible + DAF pre-exit
→ Possible: CRT at sale, §1031 if real estate component
→ Reference: `strategies/INSTALLMENT-SALE-STRUCTURE.md`, `strategies/QSBS-SECTION-1202.md`

**Family office / multi-generational wealth**
→ Primary: CLT + PRIVATE-FOUNDATION + §1202 ecosystem + CRT-CRUT
→ Possible: AIRCRAFT for family business, OIL-GAS for high-income year, FOREIGN-GILTI
→ Reference: `industries/INVESTMENT-FIRMS.md` (closest fit; family office not separate playbook)

**High-income doctor / professional (W-2 + passive investments)**
→ Primary: Retirement plan optimization (core) + EQUIPMENT-LEASING-FUNDS + DAF-ADVANCED
→ Possible: QOZ if capital gain, OIL-GAS if active participation
→ Reference: `industries/DOCTORS-MEDICAL.md`

### Client-facing framing (later, in memo form)

Do NOT hand client the 13-strategy list. Present the 2-3 top strategies with:
- What it does (plain language)
- Why it fits this client's situation
- What it costs (fees, illiquidity, complexity)
- What it yields (tax savings, wealth transfer, other)
- What the timeline looks like
- What the risks are
- What the next steps would be

## Anti-patterns (what NOT to do)

- **Do not** walk a client through all 13 strategies exhaustively. You're the filter, not an encyclopedia.
- **Do not** recommend a strategy without the prerequisite gate checks.
- **Do not** recommend aircraft, oil/gas, equipment leasing, or movie tax credits without partner review — these have elevated audit profiles.
- **Do not** present strategies as a menu. Lead with the recommendation.
- **Do not** combine too many strategies at once (>3 strategies in one engagement is complexity overload for client).
- **Do not** execute any of these — Priceless coordinates, specialists execute. Every strategy has separate specialist engagement (attorney, trustee, fund sponsor, QI, broker).
- **Do not** skip the industry playbook reference — industry context is often decisive.
