# Car Washes Industry Playbook

Priceless CPA's car wash niche — express exterior car washes (tunnel washes), full-service, self-service, and mobile operators. Growing PE rollup activity in the space; real-estate-heavy tax profile.

## Industry characteristics

**Revenue model**: Pay-per-wash + Unlimited Wash Club (subscription, $20-$40/month) for express washes. Full-service adds labor-intensive interior cleaning and detailing. Strong recurring revenue profile with subscription model.

**Cost structure**: Labor 20-30%, chemicals 8-15%, water/utilities 5-10%, credit card processing 2-4%, maintenance 5-10%, lease/mortgage 10-20%, other overhead 5-10%.

**Margin profile**: Gross 55-75%, EBITDA 30-50% for well-run express washes.

**Real estate**: Land parcels typically 0.75-2 acres. Building + tunnel equipment + canopy + vacuum pods. High capital intensity but cost seg heavily rewards §1245 classification.

**Growth pattern**: Single-location operator → multi-location portfolio. PE rollup activity means Priceless clients may be acquisition targets; tax planning matters for exit.

## Critical: Cost Segregation value

Car washes are ONE OF THE BEST cost segregation candidates in the economy.

**Typical breakdown for express wash**:
- Land: 10-20% (not depreciable)
- Building structure (§1250): 15-30% (39-year)
- Tunnel equipment (§1245): 40-55% (typically 5-7 year)
- Land improvements (§1250 improvements): 10-20% (15-year)

**Compare to typical commercial**: 70-80% §1250 / 20-30% reclassified.

Car washes: 40-55% §1245 immediately, plus 10-20% land improvements at 15-year.

**Combined with 100% permanent bonus depreciation (post-OBBBA Jan 19, 2025+)**: Year 1 deduction can be 40-55%+ of total investment (excluding land).

**Example**: $3M express wash construction on owned land:
- Land: $500K (not depreciable)
- Building structure: $700K → 39-year straight-line
- Tunnel equipment (§1245): $1.3M → 100% bonus year 1
- Land improvements (§1250 improvements): $500K → 15-year, 100% bonus year 1

Year 1 deduction: $1.3M + $500K = $1.8M of $3M investment.

This is why car washes are attractive to high-income investors — first-year deductions can fully offset significant income.

## Key tax pain points

### 1. Tunnel equipment classification

- Conveyors, wash arches, blowers, chemical applicators: §1245 personal property
- Pumps, pressure systems, vacuums, POS equipment: §1245
- Water reclamation systems: typically §1245 or 15-year improvement depending on installation

**Common error**: Treating all tunnel and equipment as building improvement (39-year). Dramatically understates depreciation.

### 2. Unlimited Wash Club subscription revenue

Typical terms: $20-$40/month for unlimited washes, billed monthly or annually.

**Revenue recognition**:
- Monthly billing: revenue recognized monthly as billed
- Annual prepay: deferred revenue liability; recognized ratably over year

**Tax treatment**: Cash basis recognizes when received. Accrual basis may differ.

**§451(b) consideration**: ASC 606 coordination for accrual taxpayers.

### 3. Credit card processing and chargebacks

High-transaction-volume business. Processing fees 2-4% of gross. Chargebacks occur; handle net or gross.

### 4. Water usage and environmental

- Water reclamation systems: often tax-incented at state level; federal cost seg opportunity
- Chemical inventory: typically operating expense (consumable)
- Environmental compliance (wastewater discharge, state variations)

### 5. Land + building + equipment financing

Often financed with:
- SBA 7(a) or SBA 504 (real estate)
- Commercial bank loan
- Equipment financing (tunnel equipment)
- Seller financing

§163(j) interest limitation considerations; post-OBBBA EBITDA basis restoration helps.

### 6. Multi-location operators

Separate entities per location typical (liability isolation).
- Partnership or LLC per location
- Common ownership through holding entity
- Management company charges to locations (transfer pricing considerations)

### 7. Exit planning

PE rollup activity means Priceless clients may be acquired. Tax-efficient exit structures:
- Asset sale vs. stock sale (asset usually better for seller if depreciation recapture manageable)
- §1031 like-kind exchange (if rolling into new location)
- Installment sale
- F-reorg to enable tax-efficient conversion pre-sale

## Strategies to run

- **Cost Segregation on every owned property** — essentially always positive NPV
- **Bonus depreciation acceleration** on acquisitions
- **QBI Optimization**: Non-SSTB, full deduction available
- **PTET election** in applicable states
- **§179D** if building energy-efficient — urgent due to June 30, 2026 cutoff
- **Water reclamation system tax incentives** at state level
- **Exit structure review** for PE rollup candidates

## Red flags

- Cost seg not done on owned properties
- Tunnel equipment classified as building improvement (39-year)
- Unlimited Wash subscription revenue not properly deferred (if annual prepay)
- Multi-location structure with no transfer pricing documentation
