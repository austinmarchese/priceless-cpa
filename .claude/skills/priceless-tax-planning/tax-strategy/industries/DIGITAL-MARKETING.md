# Digital Marketing Agencies Industry Playbook

Priceless CPA's digital marketing niche — paid media agencies, content/SEO agencies, web development shops, growth marketing firms, full-service marketing agencies.

## Industry characteristics

**Revenue model**: Monthly retainers, project-based fees, performance-based/rev-share, media commissions.

**Cost structure**: People-heavy (50-65% labor). Low capex. Often high ad-spend pass-through if buying on client's behalf.

**Margin profile**: Net 15-35%. Compression when competing on price.

**Tax character**: SSTB classification depends on service mix. Consulting/advisory = SSTB. Execution, product, proprietary tools = non-SSTB. Critical analysis area.

## Critical SSTB analysis

§199A(d)(2) SSTB categories include "consulting" — ambiguous for marketing agencies.

**More likely SSTB (consulting)**:
- Strategic advisory, marketing consulting without execution
- Agencies charging primarily for expertise/recommendations
- Fractional CMO services

**More likely non-SSTB (execution/product)**:
- Media buying with actual campaign management
- Creative production (video, design, copy as deliverable)
- SEO execution (content creation, link building, technical implementation)
- Paid media management with performance responsibility
- Proprietary platform/software offering

**Mixed agencies**: Analyze services by revenue mix. Majority determines classification.

**Documentation**: Service descriptions, engagement letters, deliverables clearly worded to reflect execution (if claiming non-SSTB).

## Key tax pain points

### 1. Ad spend pass-through

**Agency model A (pass-through)**: Client pays media vendors directly or reimburses agency at cost. Agency earns management fee only.
- Revenue = management fee only
- Ad spend not in revenue
- Cleaner P&L

**Agency model B (agency of record)**: Agency contracts with media vendor, invoices client. Agency earns margin on ad spend.
- Revenue = total billed to client (including ad spend)
- Ad spend in COGS
- Higher topline, same economic margin
- Affects gross receipts thresholds for various tax provisions

**Consistency**: Whatever model used, apply consistently. Mid-year changes complicate.

### 2. Contractor vs. employee classification

Creative talent often works for multiple agencies. Classification:
- True freelancer: 1099 (own business, multiple clients, own tools)
- De facto employee: W-2 (exclusive, agency-controlled, long-term)

§530 safe harbor available if consistent treatment + reasonable basis. Heavy IRS focus area.

### 3. R&D Credit for MarTech development

Agencies building proprietary platforms, automation tools, dashboard products — often qualify for §41 R&D credit.

Examples: reporting automation platform, attribution modeling tools, programmatic media buying tech.

**Often missed**: Agency CPAs don't see agency as "R&D" company. Opportunity.

### 4. International / foreign clients

US-based agency serving foreign client:
- Income generally US-source (services performed in US)
- No foreign withholding typically
- Foreign tax considerations in client's country (may need 8233 or W-8BEN-E)
- State sourcing varies

### 5. Deferred revenue on retainers

Monthly retainer prepaid by client → deferred revenue on accrual books. Cash-basis tax return recognizes when cash received.

Timing differences can be material for agencies with annual prepaid retainers. §451(b) coordination.

## Strategies to run

Similar to software/AI for tech-heavy agencies; similar to professional services for consulting-heavy agencies.

- **SSTB classification memo** for agencies near the line
- **QBI Optimization** if non-SSTB
- **Reasonable comp** for S Corp owners
- **Retirement plan** (Solo 401(k) or group 401(k) for team)
- **R&D credit** if building proprietary tools
- **PTET election** in applicable states
- **Multi-state nexus** for remote team

## Red flags

- SSTB classification unanalyzed (assumed non-SSTB without basis)
- Ad spend model inconsistent
- Creative freelancers treated as 1099 with employee-level control
- No R&D credit despite MarTech platform development
- Client retainer accounting not reconciling cash to accrual
