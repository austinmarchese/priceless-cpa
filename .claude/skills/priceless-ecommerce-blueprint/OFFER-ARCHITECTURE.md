**Old motion (leaks deals):**

**New motion (two one-call closes):**
1. **Call 1 (Sales):** Present ONE standardized paid offer, the E-Commerce Financial & Tax Blueprint. Close it on the call. Take payment. No proposal sent.
2. **Delivery of Blueprint:** They feel the value (they finally see their real profit, their cash flow, their tax exposure).
3. **Call 2 (Delivery/Upsell):** They ask "can you just run this for me?" Present the ongoing Quarterback program. Close on that call.

The Blueprint IS the proposal killer. You stop selling a promise on a proposal and start selling a paid, tangible deliverable you can close in the room.

### BLUEPRINT CREDIT MECHANIC
- The client pays the Blueprint fee upfront (banded $1,500 / $2,500 / $3,500).
- **If they enroll in any ongoing tier within 30 days of Blueprint delivery, 100% of the Blueprint fee is credited toward the final invoice(s) of their first 12-month term.**
- The credit **vests only if they complete the 12-month term.** Cancel early = forfeited (or prorated, firm's call). This is the lock-in.
- Applied to the LAST invoice(s), working backward until the credit is used up.

| They paid (Blueprint) | Enroll in | Credit applied as |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| $1,500 (Lite) | Tier 1 ($800/qtr) | Final quarter free ($800) + $700 off the prior quarter (~last 1.75 quarters of advisory covered) |
| $2,500 (Standard) | Tier 2 ($1,600/mo) | ~Last 1.5 months free (all of month 12 + ~half of month 11) |
| $3,500 (Complex) | Tier 3 ($2,850/mo) | Last month free ($2,850) + ~$650 off month 11 |

**Why final period, not first:** (1) kills the "you're charging me twice" objection without discounting the cash collected upfront, (2) preserves full year-one cash flow, (3) locks the full term. Crediting month 1 would just be a discount that surrenders both the lock-in and year-one cash.

**Why it protects margin:** the Blueprint band scales with business size, and size drives the tier, so a $3,500 credit only ever lands on a $27K/yr Tier 3 client (~13%). The credit is always a sensible slice of a much larger engagement.

**The one lever to change:** if the ongoing close (not the Blueprint) ever proves to be the harder step, move the credit to the FIRST period instead. More conversion, less lock-in and less year-one cash. Start with final-period; switch only if the data says to.

## PHASED ROLLOUT PLAN

### Phase 1: Prove the Blueprint (first 30-90 days)
- Retire the $400 consulting and the ad-hoc tax plan into ONE standardized Blueprint, banded $1,500 / $2,500 / $3,500.
- You run every sales call. Close the Blueprint on the call, no proposals.
- Include a guarantee for cold FB-ad traffic once ads are live: "If we don't find at least your Blueprint fee in savings or missed profit, we refund it." Referral/organic leads don't need it.
- Target: 10-20 Blueprints sold and delivered. Track close rate, how many convert Blueprint -> ongoing, and objections.

### Phase 2: Scale Recurring (after 10-20 Blueprints)
- Validate Tier 2 ($1,600/mo) and Tier 3 ($2,850/mo) against real delivery cost + margin (modeled at ~67% / ~70% on your rates); nudge up as proof builds. This is where "double, mostly recurring" gets built.
- Reduce/remove the guarantee once you have proof and testimonials.
- Systematize the Blueprint delivery so it's repeatable and fast (template the deliverable).
- Push the Blueprint -> ongoing conversion rate. This is your recurring-revenue engine.

### Phase 3: Expand (consistent results on cold traffic)
- Add a seasonal tax-prep entry offer for Jan-March (easy yes, converts to advisory).
- Consider a second closer / salesperson once volume outpaces you (your stated bottleneck).
- Scale FB ad spend against the proven Blueprint offer.
- Don't change offer + traffic + team all at once. One variable at a time.
