## Part 2: Questions to Ask Your CPA (What We Give Them)

These are the questions they should ask their CPA before signing their return. We show 7 questions, personalized based on their answers above.

### Core Questions (Everyone Gets These)

#### 1. Proactive Planning
**Question:** "What tax planning conversations have you initiated with me this year?"

**Why it matters:** A proactive CPA reaches out before deadlines, not after. If they only talk to you at tax time, you're getting compliance, not strategy.

---

#### 2. Strategies Implemented
**Question:** "What strategies did you implement this year that reduced my tax liability?"

**Why it matters:** Your CPA should be able to point to specific moves they made—not just filing your return accurately.

---

#### 3. Tax Projection
**Question:** "What's my projected tax liability for this year, and what's the plan to minimize it?"

**Why it matters:** If they can't give you a number and a plan, they're not doing strategic planning.

---

#### 4. Entity Structure
**Question:** "Is my current entity structure still optimal, or should we restructure?"

**Why it matters:** Entity structure should evolve as you grow. What worked at $300K might be costing you at $1M+.

---

#### 5. S-Corp Salary
**Question:** "Am I paying myself the optimal salary from my S-corp?"

**Why it matters:** Too high = excess payroll tax. Too low = audit risk. The right number saves thousands.

---

#### 6. Retirement Vehicles
**Question:** "What retirement vehicles should I be using to reduce taxable income?"

**Why it matters:** Solo 401(k), SEP IRA, defined benefit plans—each has different limits and benefits based on your situation.

---

#### 7. QBI Deduction
**Question:** "Am I maximizing my QBI (Qualified Business Income) deduction?"

**Why it matters:** The 20% QBI deduction has income limits and rules that vary by business type. Are you getting the full benefit?

---

### Real Estate Questions (For RE Investors)

*Shown to people who selected real estate investments or "business + RE"*

#### Cost Segregation
**Question:** "Have you done a cost segregation study on my properties?"

**Why it matters:** Cost segregation can accelerate depreciation and save tens of thousands in taxes on investment properties.

---

#### Real Estate Professional Status
**Question:** "Do I qualify for Real Estate Professional status, and are we taking advantage of it?"

**Why it matters:** REP status lets you use real estate losses against ordinary income—a massive benefit if you qualify.

---

#### 1031 Exchanges
**Question:** "What's the plan if I sell a property? Have we discussed 1031 exchanges?"

**Why it matters:** Selling without a plan can trigger a huge tax bill. A 1031 exchange can defer it entirely.

---

### Multi-Entity Questions (For Complex Structures)

*Shown to people with 3+ entities or "it's complicated"*

#### Entity Coordination
**Question:** "Are my entities structured to minimize overall tax liability and liability exposure?"

**Why it matters:** Multiple entities should work together strategically, not just exist independently.

---

#### Intercompany Agreements
**Question:** "Are we using intercompany agreements and management fees appropriately?"

**Why it matters:** Properly structured intercompany transactions can shift income to lower-tax entities.

---

### High Income Questions (For $1M+)

*Shown to people with $1M+ income*

#### Charitable Strategy
**Question:** "Should I be using a Donor Advised Fund or charitable strategy?"

**Why it matters:** At higher income levels, strategic charitable giving can provide significant tax benefits while supporting causes you care about.

---

#### Estate Coordination
**Question:** "How is my tax planning coordinating with estate planning?"

**Why it matters:** At your wealth level, tax and estate planning should work together. Are your advisors talking to each other?
