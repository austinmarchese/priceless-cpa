## Why This Works

Loss aversion is the strongest psychological driver. Entrepreneurs who've built $5-50M in wealth fear losing it more than they desire gaining more. "51% do this" in the thumbnail adds a data hook and creates urgency: am I in that 51%? Contrarian angle since most tax content promises how to get rich, not how the rich lose it.
