## Summary: 6 Patterns Ranked by Proven Performance

### 1. IRS Fear/Urgency (HIGHEST VIEWS)
- "IRS Cracking Down on Self-Employment Income (Avoid These Mistakes!)" (387K, 8.1x)
- "What Actually Triggers an IRS Audit" (293K, 4.7x)
- **Why it dominates:** Fear + loss aversion. The IRS is the boogeyman for every entrepreneur. These are the highest raw view counts in the dataset. Audit anxiety is explicitly called out in the persona profile.

### 2. "How the Rich [Do X]" (HIGHEST MULTIPLIERS)
- "How the Rich Sell Crypto Without Paying Taxes" (266K, 13.2x)
- "Why the Rich Put Everything in a Living Trust (And You Should Too!)" (199K, 10.8x)
- "How the Rich Use LLCs to Hide Their Assets" (59K, 4.6x)
- "How to Offset Taxes With Real Estate" (89K, 3.8x) -- thumbnail says "THE RICH DO THIS"
- "How the Rich Use Trusts to Avoid Tax (Legally)" (45K, 1.1x)
- "How the Rich Use the Trifecta to Pay Less in Taxes" (22K, 2.1x)
- **Why it dominates:** 6 of 19 outliers use this framing. Kohler built his entire recent strategy on it. Aspiration bridge + insider knowledge. The persona has FOMO about what other entrepreneurs are doing smarter.

### 3. Bold Time-for-Money Promise
- "Give Me 8min and I'll Save You 50k in Tax" (244K, 6.4x)
- **Why it works:** Asymmetric value exchange. Low time commitment, massive dollar promise. Only one video uses it, but 6.4x proves the pattern. Matches how the persona evaluates everything: ROI on time spent.

### 4. "How to Write Off [Desirable Thing] (Legally)"
- "How to Write Off Travel (Legally)" (172K, 4.8x)
- "How to Write Off Your Car (Legally)" (92K)
- "How to Turn Everyday Expenses into Tax Write Offs in 2026!" (32K, 4.0x)
- **Why it works:** The viewer already wants the thing (travel, car, meals). The title adds a tax benefit on top. "(Legally)" adds safety. Great top-of-funnel content that leads to "what else is my CPA missing?"

### 5. Family + Tax Strategy
- "Can You Avoid Taxes by Paying Your Kids?" (80K, 2.8x)

- "5 Wealth Building Rules Rich Families Teach Their Kids Early" (37K, 1.1x)

### 6. Elite Structures Made Accessible
- "How to Set Up a Family Office Without Being Ultra-Rich" (13K, 8.6x)
- "Why Smart Investors Love Health Savings Accounts" (23K, 2.4x)
- "How the Rich Use the Trifecta to Pay Less in Taxes" (22K, 2.1x)
