# 7 Questions Lead Magnet — First 3

**Format:** Challenge / Lead Magnet
**Topic:** CPA evaluation
**Target:** Serial entrepreneur wondering if their CPA is actually good
**Duration:** ~55 seconds
**Status:** Ready to film

---

## Script

Want to know if your CPA is actually doing their job?

I'm going to give you 3 questions. If they can't answer these, you might have a problem.

Question one: What strategies did you implement this year that reduced my tax liability?

Not what deductions they found. What did they *implement*? What moves did they make proactively?

If they can't name specific strategies, they're not planning. They're filing.

Question two: What's my projected tax liability for next year?

A real tax strategist knows what you're going to owe before April. They're not waiting to find out. They're planning for it.

If they don't have a number, they're reactive — not proactive.

Question three: Is my current entity structure still optimal for my income level?

What worked at $300K doesn't work at a million. If they've never brought this up, they're not watching your growth.

These are just 3 of the 7 questions you should be asking.

If you want all 7, comment "CPA" and I'll send you the full list.
