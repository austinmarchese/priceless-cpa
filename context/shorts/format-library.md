# Short-Form Content Format Library

Reference for YouTube Shorts/Reels/TikTok formats that work for serial entrepreneur audience.

---

## Format Overview

| Format | Description | Best For | Persona Fit |
|--------|-------------|----------|-------------|
| **Contrarian Take** | Challenge common practice | Tax myths, CPA industry problems | High |
| **Problem Warning** | "The mistake that's costing you..." | Entity structure, common errors | High |
| **POV Reveal** | "POV: Your CPA just told you..." | Outcomes, transformations | High |
| **Before/After** | Show the transformation | Strategy results, client stories | High |
| **Challenge** | "I reviewed X returns and found..." | Data-driven insights | Medium |
| **B-Roll Storytelling** | Narrative over visuals | Personal brand, expertise building | Medium |
| **Split-Screen Reaction** | React to tax news/advice | Trending topics, bad advice | Medium |

---

## Format Details

### 1. Contrarian Take Format

**Structure:**
1. Bold contrarian statement (0-3s)
2. Common belief context (3-15s)
3. Your reasoning (15-45s)
4. Actionable insight (45-60s)

**Hook Templates:**
- "Stop [common practice] — here's why it's costing you thousands"
- "Your CPA probably told you to [X]. That's wrong."
- "The tax advice everyone gives is backwards"
- "Here's what your CPA isn't telling you about [topic]"

**Best Topics:**
- Generic CPA vs. strategic CPA
- Common entity structure mistakes
- Tax prep vs. tax strategy
- Reactive vs. proactive planning
- Outdated tax advice

**Example Opening:**
> "Your CPA told you to maximize your S-corp salary. That advice might be costing you $30K a year."

---

### 2. Problem Warning Format

**Structure:**
1. State the problem clearly (0-3s)
2. Why entrepreneurs make this mistake (3-15s)
3. What it costs them (15-35s)
4. How to avoid it (35-60s)

**Hook Templates:**
- "The #1 mistake I see with entrepreneurs making $1M+"
- "This entity structure mistake is costing you thousands"
- "If you have [situation], you're probably overpaying"
- "The tax trap that catches every real estate investor"

**Best Topics:**
- Wrong entity structure for income level
- Missing the QBI deduction
- S-corp salary set incorrectly
- Not doing quarterly projections
- Advisors not talking to each other

**Example Opening:**
> "If you're making over a million and still have the same entity structure from when you made 200K, you're leaving serious money on the table."

---

### 3. POV Reveal Format

**Structure:**
1. "POV: [situation]" (0-2s)
2. Show the outcome/realization (2-15s)
3. Brief context (15-40s)
4. What changed/insight (40-60s)

**Hook Templates:**
- "POV: Your CPA just found you $47K in tax savings"
- "POV: You finally asked your CPA the right question"
- "POV: You realized your accountant has no idea what a cost seg is"
- "POV: Your tax projection came back and you're not scrambling"

**Best Topics:**
- Client transformation stories (anonymized)
- The "aha moment" of proactive planning
- Discovering you've been overpaying
- What quarterly planning feels like

**Example Opening:**
> "POV: You asked your CPA 'what strategies did you implement this year?' and they couldn't name one."

---

### 4. Before/After Format

**Structure:**
1. The "before" situation (0-5s)
2. Quick transition (5-10s)
3. The "after" result (10-40s)
4. How it happened (40-60s)

**Hook Templates:**
- "Client came to me paying $X. Now they pay $Y."
- "Before: panicking in April. After: knowing in November."
- "What changed when we restructured their entities"
- "Tax season used to be [bad]. Now it's [good]."

**Best Topics:**
- Entity restructuring results
- Proactive vs. reactive comparison
- Tax liability reductions
- Client experience transformation

**Example Opening:**
> "This client came to me paying $180K in taxes. After restructuring, they're paying $95K — on the same income."

---

### 5. Challenge Format

**Structure:**
1. Set up the challenge/analysis (0-3s)
2. Show what you found (3-45s)
3. Reveal the insight (45-60s)

**Hook Templates:**
- "I reviewed 50 entrepreneur tax returns. Here's what I found."
- "I looked at every S-corp client over $1M. The pattern is clear."
- "I asked 10 CPAs about [topic]. Only 2 got it right."

**Best Topics:**
- Common patterns in high-income returns
- Industry-wide mistakes
- What separates good from great tax outcomes

**Example Opening:**
> "I reviewed 50 returns from entrepreneurs making over a million. 80% were paying more than they should."

---

### 6. B-Roll Storytelling Format

**Structure:**
1. Hook line over interesting visual (0-3s)
2. Story narration over B-roll (3-50s)
3. Insight/lesson (50-60s)

**Hook Templates:**
- "I just got off a call with a client who..."
- "Here's what happened when I asked my client about..."
- "The most expensive mistake I've seen this year..."

**Visual Ideas:**
- Office shots, laptop, coffee
- Client meeting setup (no faces)
- Reviewing documents, spreadsheets
- Walking and talking

**Best Topics:**
- Client stories (anonymized)
- Personal philosophy on tax strategy
- Industry observations
- Building trust and expertise

---

### 7. Split-Screen Reaction Format

**Structure:**
1. Show the clip/advice (0-2s)
2. Your reaction begins (2-5s)
3. Commentary and correction (5-50s)
4. Right approach (50-60s)

**Hook Templates:**
- "This tax advice is going viral — and it's wrong"
- "I can't believe a CPA said this..."
- "Let me fix this real quick..."

**Best Topics:**
- Bad tax advice on social media
- Common misconceptions
- Oversimplified strategies
- News about tax law changes

## Persona Filter: Would They Watch?

Before choosing a format, ask:

| Question | Good Answer |
|----------|-------------|
| Does this apply to $250K-$3M+ income? | Yes |
| Does this address complex situations? | Yes |
| Is this beyond basic/Google-able? | Yes |
| Does it make Anthony the expert? | Yes |
| Would they share this with a peer? | Yes |
| Does it position proactive as the solution? | Yes |

## Topic-to-Format Quick Guide

| Topic | Best Format | Why |
|-------|-------------|-----|
| "Your CPA should..." | Contrarian Take | Direct challenge to status quo |
| Entity structure | Problem Warning | Common mistake, high cost |
| Cost segregation | Before/After or POV | Show the transformation |
| S-corp salary | Problem Warning | Technical but impactful |
| QBI deduction | Contrarian Take | Most don't maximize it |
| Quarterly projections | POV Reveal | Show what "proactive" feels like |
| Real estate + business | Problem Warning | Complex, often mishandled |
| Retirement vehicles | Challenge | Data-driven comparison |

---

## What to Avoid

- **Too basic** — If they can Google it, skip it
- **Hard sells** — Don't pitch services, position expertise
- **Jargon without context** — Explain terms briefly
- **Fear-based** — Focus on opportunity, not anxiety
- **Too broad** — One idea per short, go deep
- **Lifestyle content** — This isn't about flexing, it's about expertise
