# Content Strategy - Serial Entrepreneur Persona

Content strategy informed by the serial entrepreneur persona.

## Content Goals

1. **Attract** — Get discovered by entrepreneurs searching for tax/wealth answers
2. **Build trust** — Demonstrate expertise and modern understanding
3. **Trigger action** — Surface pain points that make them realize they need to switch
4. **Enable referrals** — Create content that's easy to share ("you need to watch this")

## Platform Strategy

| Platform | Purpose | Content Type |
|----------|---------|--------------|
| **YouTube** | Deep trust building, SEO | Long-form explanations, strategy breakdowns |
| **Instagram** | Awareness, shareability | Short-form clips, carousels, stories |
| **TikTok** | Reach, virality | Quick hits, myth-busting, hot takes |
| **Referral content** | Arm clients to share | "Send this to your entrepreneur friend who..." |

## Content Pillars

### 1. Tax Strategies (The Hook)
Specific tactics that make them think "my CPA never told me this."

**Topics:**
- Cost segregation for real estate investors
- Qualified Opportunity Zones
- S-corp vs LLC for entrepreneurs
- Multi-entity tax optimization
- State tax strategies for remote businesses
- Crypto tax planning
- Exit planning and tax implications

**Angle:** "Here's a strategy that could save you $X — and why your CPA probably hasn't mentioned it."

### 2. Mistakes to Avoid (The Wake-Up Call)
Content that surfaces the pain of their current setup.

**Topics:**
- Signs your CPA doesn't understand your business
- Entity structure mistakes that cost entrepreneurs thousands
- What happens when your advisors don't talk to each other
- Tax planning mistakes before a big exit
- Why "filing taxes" isn't the same as "tax strategy"

**Angle:** "If this sounds familiar, you might be leaving money on the table."

### 3. The Quarterback Model (The Differentiator)
Why the unified approach beats fragmented advisors.

**Topics:**
- What a proactive CPA relationship actually looks like
- End-to-end financial management for entrepreneurs
- How to coordinate bookkeeping, tax, and strategy
- What to expect from a CPA who "gets it"
- The cost of scattered finances (time, money, risk)

**Angle:** "This is what it looks like when someone's actually quarterbacking your finances."

### 4. Asset Protection & Privacy (The Next Level)
For entrepreneurs who've built enough to protect.

**Topics:**
- Entity structures for asset protection
- Privacy strategies for business owners
- Trust structures for entrepreneurs
- Protecting business assets from liability
- Estate planning basics for $5M+ net worth

**Angle:** "Once you've built something worth protecting, here's how to protect it."

### 5. Wealth Building (The Big Picture)
Bigger picture content that positions Anthony as a strategic thinker.

**Topics:**
- Tax-efficient wealth building strategies
- Real estate + business owner tax optimization
- Building generational wealth as an entrepreneur
- When to reinvest vs. extract from your business
- Long-term tax planning, not just annual filing

**Angle:** "Tax strategy is wealth strategy. Here's the big picture."

## Content Formats

### YouTube (Long-Form)
- **Strategy deep-dives** — 10-20 min explanations of specific tactics
- **Mistake breakdowns** — "5 tax mistakes costing entrepreneurs thousands"
- **Case studies** — "How we saved this client $X" (anonymized)
- **Q&A / Ask me anything** — Address common questions

### Short-Form (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts)
- **Myth busting** — "Your CPA told you X, but actually..."
- **Quick tips** — One actionable insight in 60 seconds
- **Hot takes** — Contrarian views on common practices
- **Day in the life** — What proactive tax planning looks like

### Carousels (Instagram)
- **Checklists** — "5 signs you need a new CPA"
- **Comparisons** — "Generic CPA vs. Quarterback CPA"
- **Step-by-step** — "How to evaluate your entity structure"

## Content Angles That Resonate

Based on persona insights:

| Pain Point | Content Angle |
|------------|---------------|
| "I'm overpaying on taxes" | "X strategies your CPA isn't using" |
| "No one sees the big picture" | "Why your advisors need to talk to each other" |
| "My CPA doesn't get modern business" | "Tax strategies for ecom/digital entrepreneurs" |

| "I want aggressive but legal" | "The line between smart tax planning and risk" |

## Trigger-Based Content

Content timed to trigger events:

| Trigger | Content |
|---------|---------|
| Tax season | "Before you file: X things to check" |
| Q4 planning | "Year-end tax moves for entrepreneurs" |
| Moving states | "State tax considerations for relocating business owners" |
| Considering exit | "Tax planning before you sell your business" |
| Heard about strategy | Deep-dive on that specific strategy |

## Call to Action Strategy

**Soft CTA:** "If this sounds like your situation, we might be able to help."

**Hard CTA:** "Book a consultation to see if we're a fit."

**Referral CTA:** "Know an entrepreneur dealing with this? Send them this video."

## Metrics That Matter

- **Reach:** Views, impressions (awareness)
- **Engagement:** Comments asking questions (intent)
- **Shares:** Content being sent to friends (referral potential)
- **Consultations booked:** Actual conversion
- **Quality of leads:** Are they the right persona?

## Content Cadence (Suggested)

- **YouTube:** 1-2 long-form per week
- **Short-form:** 3-5 per week across platforms
- **Carousels:** 1-2 per week

Consistency > volume. Better to do less at higher quality.
