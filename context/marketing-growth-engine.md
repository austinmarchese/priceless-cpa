```
                    ┌─→ [7 Questions Tool] ─→ [Landing Page] ─┐

[Facebook Ads] ─────┼─→ [Landing Page] ──────────────────────┘
[Referrals] ────────┴─→ [Free Tax Evaluation] ───────────────→
```

### Lead Magnet A: 7 Questions Tool (Cold Traffic)
**"Before You Sign: 7 Questions to Ask Your CPA About Your Return"**

This is an **interactive website** (not a PDF). User answers questions → gets personalized questions to ask their CPA.

**Distribution paths:**
- **Instagram organic:** Video covers 3 mind-blowing questions → "Comment 'TAX' for 5 more" → **ManyChat DMs the link** to 7 Questions Tool
- **TikTok/Shorts:** Same content, link in bio to 7 Questions Tool
- **Facebook Ads:** Can go to 7 Questions Tool OR directly to Landing Page (for warmer audiences)

### Lead Magnet B: Free Tax Evaluation (Referral Traffic)
**High-value offer for warm referrals**

- Exclusive to referrals from top clients
- Premium positioning — "My accountant is offering free evaluations to his best clients' networks"
- Goes straight to sales call (these are warm, high-trust leads)

### Landing Page
For people ready to book — captures info for sales call
