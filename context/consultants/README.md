# Consultants

Cloned experts whose frameworks you can apply at scale.

## What Goes Here

Expert frameworks you've extracted from:
- Copywriters you admire
- Marketing strategists
- Content creators in the accounting space
- Anyone whose thinking you want to apply

## How to Add a Consultant

Run `/create-consultant` to be guided through the process.

Or manually create: `consultant-name/frameworks.md`

## How AI Uses This

When reviewing content or giving strategic feedback, AI applies these expert frameworks—like having a team of specialists on call.

## Suggested Consultants to Clone

- **A direct-response copywriter** — For landing page and email copy
- **A content strategist** — For blog and SEO content
- **An industry expert** — Someone who knows accounting content specifically
