# Lived Experiences

Your accumulated wisdom about content that works. Every entry makes AI smarter about what converts for Priceless CPA.

## What Goes Here

- Content that performed well (and why)
- Client feedback that changed your approach
- Industry insights from real conversations
- A/B test results and learnings
- Mistakes that taught you something

## How to Add Entries

New entries are captured via [[.claude/skills/daily-journal/SKILL.md|/daily-journal]] at the end of a content session or client call.

Or manually create a file: `YYYY-MM-DD-brief-description.md`
