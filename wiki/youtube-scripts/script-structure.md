## INTRO STRUCTURE (Under 60 Seconds)

**Formula: Context > Common Belief > Contrarian > Proof > Plan**

### 1. Immediate Context (5-10s)
- Confirm the click. Viewer knows they're in the right place.
- Call out exactly who should watch. Make them feel seen.

### 2. Common Belief (10-15s)
- "Most people think..." / "Everyone says..."
- Establish what the audience already believes.

### 3. Contrarian Take (10-15s)
- "But here's the thing..."
- Stun them with an unexpected perspective.
- Use "but" as a scroll-stop interjection.

### 4. Proof/Credibility (10-15s)
- Brief credibility marker. Anthony's experience, client results, or a specific number that earns trust.
- Example: "I've restructured over 40 entities this year alone, and every single one of them was leaving money on the table."

### 5. Plan (10-15s)
- "By the end of this video, you'll know exactly..."
- Include a guarantee if natural.
- Be specific about the outcome, not vague or aspirational.

## INTRO EXAMPLES (Tax/Accounting Topics)

**Example 1 (Entity Structuring):**
> "If you own more than one business and you're running everything through the same entity, we need to talk. Most CPAs will tell you it's fine, keep it simple. But here's the thing: that 'simplicity' is costing you tens of thousands every year in missed deductions and unnecessary tax exposure. I restructured 40+ entities this past year, and every single owner said the same thing: 'Why didn't my CPA bring this up sooner?' By the end of this video, you'll know exactly how to tell if your current structure is bleeding money, and the three changes that fix it."

**Example 2 (Real Estate + Business Owner):**
> "You built your business, started investing in real estate, and now your tax situation is a mess. Most entrepreneurs think their CPA has it handled. But here's what I see all the time: your CPA is filing returns, not building strategy. That's not a strategy. That's compliance. I work with business owners making $500K to $3M who own rental properties, and the patterns are always the same. Three specific moves that nobody brings up until it's too late. Let me walk you through all three."

**Example 3 (Tax Savings / Loss Aversion):**
> "You made $1.2M last year across two businesses. Your CPA sent you a bill for $387K in taxes and said 'good year.' But here's the thing: $147K of that was avoidable. Not aggressive. Not gray area. Legitimate strategies that your CPA either doesn't know or doesn't have time to implement. I'm going to show you the five strategies that would have saved that $147K, and exactly how to bring them up with your CPA this week."

## BODY STRUCTURE

### Core Principles
- Front-load value. Give immediate payoff.
- Each point must be BETTER than the last (ascending value trend).
- Re-hook every 60-90 seconds (see [[wiki/youtube-scripts/voice-patterns.md]] for re-hook formulas).
- Each major point follows: What > Why > How (the Value Loop).

### Value Loop Structure
1. **What it is** - Clear, concise, digestible (context)
2. **Why it matters** - Zoom out, fit in bigger picture (framing)
3. **How to do it** - Clear, articulate, relevant examples (application)

### Point Template
```
[RE-HOOK if not first point]
"Now that point was important, but if you don't combine it with this next one, you're missing the full picture..."

[WHAT IT IS - Clear statement]
[ONE SENTENCE explaining what it is]

[WHY IT MATTERS - Context]
[Why this is important / what it costs when done wrong. USE SPECIFIC NUMBERS.]

[HOW TO DO IT - Application]
Here's exactly what this looks like:
1. [First step - specific action]
2. [Second step - specific action]
3. [Third step - specific action]

[EXAMPLE - Client story or lived experience]
"I had a client making $1.2M across two businesses..." [WALKTHROUGH]

[RESULT/PROOF]
"And just like that, [OUTCOME with specific number]."
```

### Client Story Integration

Every major point should include a client story when possible. Structure:

1. **Setup**: Brief, anonymized context. "$1.2M across two businesses, three rental properties."
2. **Problem**: What was going wrong. "His CPA had everything running through one S-Corp."
3. **Fix**: What Anthony did differently. "We separated the real estate into an LLC, elected..."
4. **Result**: Specific number. "Saved $47K in year one. $47K. From a restructure that took two weeks."

The authenticity test: "Could any CPA have written this?" If yes, it needs a lived experience or specific client story to make it uniquely Anthony's.

## COMMON MISTAKES (Optional Section)

If the topic has clear pitfalls, include a mistakes section. Structure each as:

1. The mistake itself (one sentence)
2. Why people make it (one sentence)
3. What it costs them (specific number or consequence)
4. What to do instead (one sentence)

At least one mistake should be "what your CPA is probably doing wrong" to reinforce Priceless CPA positioning.

## CLOSE STRUCTURE

### Formula: Recap Categories + ONE Action Item + Soft CTA + Tease

```
[QUICK RECAP - Categories, not every detail]
"All right, let me bring this all together:
[Category 1]: [Key takeaway]
[Category 2]: [Key takeaway]
[Category 3]: [Key takeaway]"

[ONE ACTION ITEM]
"Here's what I'd tell you if you were sitting across from me right now:
[Single, specific, actionable thing they can do this week]"

[SOFT CTA]
"If you want help implementing this, link's in the description."

[TEASE RELATED VIDEO]
"And if you want to go deeper on [related topic], click here. I break down [what the next video covers]."
```

### Closing Don'ts
- Don't summarize every point (wasted time)
- Don't beg for likes/subscribes
- Don't apologize for length
- Don't add new information
- Don't have multiple endings
- Don't be overly sentimental

## OUTLIER PATTERNS

Match every video to a proven pattern from outlier research before scripting.

| Pattern | Structure | When to Use |
|---------|-----------|-------------|
| Aspiration Bridge | "How To [Action] As [Identity]" | Actionable how-tos |
| Contrarian/Status Threat | "Why You Should NOT [Popular Thing]" | Challenging common advice |
| Loss Aversion | "[Life Event]? Avoid This [Bad Outcome]" | Specific mistakes with stakes |
| Inclusion + Hidden Money | "[Number] [Benefit] for [Broad Group]" | Listicle-style content |
| Authority Takedown | "CPA Reacts to [Bad Advice]" | Debunking/reaction content |
| Newsjacking | "[News Event]. What This Means For You" | Timely content |
