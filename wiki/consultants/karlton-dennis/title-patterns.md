## Pattern Breakdown

### 1. Aspiration + How-To (5.5M views peak)
The highest-performing pattern. Combines something the viewer wants to do with a clear promise of instruction.

**Formula:** "How To [Desired Outcome] As/With [Structure]"

**Why it works:** The viewer already wants to do this. The title just promises to show them how.

### 2. Contrarian (1.9M views peak)
Tells the viewer NOT to do something they assumed was smart.

**Formula:** "Why You Should NOT [Common Advice]!"

**Why it works:** Creates immediate cognitive dissonance. If everyone says to do X, why shouldn't I?

### 3. Loss Aversion (1.7M, 1.3M views)
The most emotionally charged pattern. Frames the content around avoiding a costly mistake.

**Formula:** "Avoid These MISTAKES BEFORE [Action]!" or "[Situation]? Avoid This Huge [Consequence]!"

**Why it works:** Fear of loss is stronger than desire for gain. People click to protect what they have.

### 4. Newsjacking (1.3M, 853K views)
Ties tax content to breaking news or policy changes. Creates urgency.

**Formula:** "[Authority] [Action] [Policy]. What This Means For You" or "[Authority] To [Dramatic Action]? FULL DETAILS"

**Why it works:** Time-sensitive content gets clicks from people who feel they need to act now.

### 5. Listicle (1.3M views)
Classic list format. Works because it promises specific, scannable value.

**Formula:** "Top [Number] [Benefit Category] For [Entity Type]"

**Why it works:** Specificity signals value. The viewer knows exactly what they're getting.

### 6. Authority (811K views)
Positions a strategy as the definitive explanation, implying others got it wrong.

**Formula:** "[Strategy]! A Proper Explanation"

**Why it works:** "Proper" implies everyone else's explanation was incomplete or incorrect.

## Application to Priceless CPA

### Patterns that map directly to Anthony's audience:

**Loss Aversion** is the strongest fit. Serial entrepreneurs with $5-50M net worth have the most to lose. They're protecting wealth, not building it from scratch. Titles like "The Tax Mistake That Costs Real Estate Investors $200K+" or "Selling Your Business? Avoid This Exit Tax Trap" would land hard.

**Aspiration + How-To** works for entity structure and tax strategy content. "How To Structure Your Real Estate Holdings for Zero Tax on Sale" speaks directly to the multi-entity entrepreneur.

**Newsjacking** applies when tax law changes hit (new brackets, proposed legislation, IRS enforcement updates). Anthony's audience needs to know what policy changes mean for their specific structures.

**Contrarian** works best when challenging advice from generalist CPAs. "Why Your CPA's Advice on S-Corp Elections Is Wrong" directly validates their frustration with reactive accountants.

### Patterns to use cautiously:

**Listicle** can feel too basic for sophisticated entrepreneurs. If used, the items need to be advanced: "5 Tax Strategies Your CPA Has Never Mentioned" rather than "Top 5 Write-Offs."

**Authority** works only when Anthony has genuine depth that others lack. "Multi-Entity Tax Planning: A Proper Explanation" works because most CPAs don't handle this well.
