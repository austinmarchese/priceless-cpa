# Best Work

Examples of content that performed well. AI learns your standards from examples, not rules.

## What Goes Here

- Landing page copy that converted
- Email sequences that got responses
- Blog posts that ranked or got shared
- Social posts that engaged
- Client communication that landed well

## How to Add Examples

Create a file with descriptive name: `landing-page-lawyers.md`, `email-sequence-q4-tax.md`

Include:
1. The actual content
2. Why it worked (if known)
3. Any metrics or feedback

## How AI Uses This

When you ask AI to write something, it matches YOUR voice and YOUR standards—not generic copy.
