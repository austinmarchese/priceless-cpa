## First Principles Behind the Outliers

### 1. Aspiration Bridge + Universal Entry Point
**Example**: "How To Pay Yourself As An LLC" (5.5M)
- Catches people at moment of peak motivation (just formed LLC)
- Promises clear answer to urgent, specific problem
- Structure: "How To [Action] As [Identity]"

### 2. Contrarian / Status Threat
**Example**: "Why You Should NOT Form an LLC!" (1.9M)
- Challenges the universal entrepreneur advice ("get an LLC")
- Creates cognitive dissonance: "Wait, am I wrong?"
- Structure: "Why You Should NOT [Thing Everyone Recommends]"

### 3. Loss Aversion + Life Event
**Example**: "Passing Your Home to Your Kids? Avoid This Huge Tax Mistake!" (1.3M)
- Targets specific life event (inheritance) with specific fear
- "Huge" amplifies stakes. "Your home" makes it personal.
- Structure: "[Life Event]? Avoid This [Magnitude] [Negative Outcome]!"

### 4. Inclusion + Hidden Money
**Example**: "9 HUGE Tax Write Offs for Individuals (EVERYONE can use these)" (558K)
- "EVERYONE" removes the "is this for me?" filter
- Broadest possible audience capture
- Structure: "[Number] [Magnitude] [Benefit] for [Broad Group] ([Qualifier])"

### 5. Controversy Magnet + Authority Takedown
**Example**: Jasmine DiLucci's entire channel (204K avg)
- Credentialed expert destroys popular advice
- Entertainment (drama) + education (correct info) combined
- Structure: "[Expert] Reacts to [Bad Advice]"

### 6. Newsjacking + Personal Impact
**Example**: "IRS Releases New Inflation Tax Brackets. What This Means For You" (1.3M)
- Ties to a news event everyone is talking about
- "What This Means For You" makes it personal
- Structure: "[News Event]. What This Means For You"
